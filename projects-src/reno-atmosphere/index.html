Sensor geometry

Distance is useful provenance—not a claim that a ground instrument measures the flat.

S109 · wind / temperature / humidity2.9–3.1 km horizontalheight changes the line by less than 2 m
High-floor separationabout 80–90 m verticallytower wakes and facade pressure dominate that small distance correction

PSI, PM2.5 and forecasts are regional products. They do not have an honest point-sensor distance from the home. The animated pressure-driven airflow is illustrative—not indoor sensing or engineering CFD.

Closed air-conditioned bedroom: use HEPA if allergies or measured particles justify it.

Cooking or renovation dust: capture and ventilate at source first.
