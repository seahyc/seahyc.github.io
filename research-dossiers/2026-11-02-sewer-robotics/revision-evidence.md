# Revision evidence pack — full-stack operator thesis
2 November2026. Revises founder decision after reading relevant existing Gemini research. Raw Gemini material is private outside this public repository. It is a lead only. No new model generation, outreach, application or fieldwork performed.

## Changed founder decision
Should a founder own a technology-enabled sewer inspection service, initially delivering one repeatable job type through competent operators and existing equipment, and fund differentiated robotics from repeat contribution? Selling software to contractors is an alternative comparator, not the assumed company boundary. Existing contractor partnerships are a learning/authorised operations path, not the ultimate imposed business model.

## Hypotheses kept and rejected
KEEP AS PROPOSAL: own delivery to control evidence quality, collect permitted outcome data and coordinate work; human-supervised service before custom robotics; lease/buy existing equipment; cash-generating service as R&D funding path. Falsify if contribution after market-rate management pay, full costs and working-capital reserve cannot finance continued operation and a bounded R&D budget, or incumbents perform as well at lower cost.
REJECT AS FACT: full-stack bypasses procurement; entire value chain becomes profit; guaranteed regulatory compliance; service margins automatically become software margins; sewers readily expand into roads/bridges/rail; tenth the cost/specific huge market figures unsupported.
