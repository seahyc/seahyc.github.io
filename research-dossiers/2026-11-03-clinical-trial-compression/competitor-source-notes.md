## 11. Singapore HSA: CTA/CTN

https://www.hsa.gov.sg/other-regulations/clinical-trials/application/apply-cta-ctn/

Official regulator page, explicitly **last updated 2026-10-31** (L25); opened, 189 extracted lines.

- Standard CTA **30 working days**; narrow Phase 1 bioequivalence/bioavailability/food-effect/drug-interaction studies **15**; Class 2 CTGTP **60**; CTN **5**.
- CTA applies to unregistered products or unapproved uses; CTN to locally registered products used according to approved labels. These are different eligibility routes, not freely chosen speed options.
- CTA may run in parallel with IRB submission; CTN follows IRB approval. Clock excludes clarification/additional-information stop time. GCP/protocol/regulatory/SOP compliance still required.
- Actual tool-read evidence L47 table `30 working days`, `15 working days`, `60 working days`, `5 working days`; L112 `excludes ‘stop-clock time’`.
- Limit: turnaround is regulatory processing, not full site startup or enrollment. No claim that software can remove these periods.

## 12. Singapore HSA: sponsor responsibility

https://www.hsa.gov.sg/other-regulations/clinical-trials/conducting-clinical-trials/sponsor/

Official regulator page; opened, 234 extracted lines; accessed 2026-11-03 (site footer updated 2026-11-02; not a document-specific publication date).

Sponsor must maintain trial quality assurance/control and oversight; local sponsor should be an ACRA-registered Singapore business entity. Transferring activities to vendors does not transfer overall responsibility. Written agreements should define activities and responsibilities before work begins; monitoring safeguards participants, reliable results and compliant conduct.

Actual excerpt L98: “retains the overall responsibility for all sponsor activities”.

Implication: automation needs traceability, oversight and validation appropriate to risk. Selling a tool does not remove sponsor accountability. This source establishes governance obligations, not a requirement that every technology vendor itself be the local sponsor.

## Synthesis and unresolved checks

**Supported inference:** the market has incumbents at every main surface: CRO execution, trial operating platforms, provider integration, real-world-data networks, recruitment and digital-twin statistics. A credible entrant needs a sharply bounded measurable bottleneck and a reason incumbents cannot deliver it equally well.

**Do not publish as facts:** a summed CRO revenue TAM; backlog as guaranteed future revenue; all trial delays caused by recruitment; record counts as available participants; vendor speed claims as independent effect sizes; HSA turnaround as total startup time.

**Missing commercial evidence:** actual buyer budget and procurement owner; cost per randomized retained participant; site integration labor and permissions; agreed baseline/clock; screen-failure and retention impact; sponsor-approved validation requirements. Next research should use scoped buyer/site interviews and a retrospective or prospective matched workflow pilot with predefined quality and timing endpoints. No interviews or outreach were conducted in this assignment.
