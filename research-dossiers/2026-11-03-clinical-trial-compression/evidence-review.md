## Calibration, bear case and falsification

Facts, reported vendor experiments, original operational inference and proposed test thresholds are visibly distinguished. The strongest bear case is present: site/data access and clinical judgment may dominate; unavoidable science and endpoint observation persist; incumbent distribution and QA can absorb the apparent savings; service customization can destroy margins; software faces integration and procurement; the base buyer economics fail despite supplier profit.

The initial wedge is a bounded, sponsor-accepted Phase II feasibility/startup-readiness package. Atopic dermatitis is a proposed discovery cohort conditional on access, not an empirically selected best market. The revised first-sellable-scope paragraph specifies deliverables and responsibilities. The proposed paid pilots measure acceptance, corrections, all labor, cash collection and downstream startup timing; critical omissions pause live use, and lack of attributable milestone movement or buyer value requires stopping/repositioning. The arbitrary investment hurdles are labeled as such.
