# Execution path · relationship-aware voice

Everything below is the author's proposed plan unless a source is cited (ids refer to evidence-ledger.md). No outreach or build has occurred.

## Sequence and gates
- **Stage 0 Personal composer** (open source, local, no sending). Proves person x channel beats thread context. Cost: founder time and model usage (assumed small; no sourced figure). Gate (source positioning.md): B >=70% of pooled blind picks and lower time-to-send/edits over a week. Falsifier: A ~ B.
- **Stage 1 Conversation audit** (paid retrospective). 100 historical WhatsApp conversations each for Moses (Respond.io Enterprise) and Repair.sg, per smb-positioning.md: status, stage, missed/late follow-up, premature pricing, suggested reply, escalation. Funds: audit fees (no price set; none sourced). Gate (source): report finds important issues the owners had not seen; suggested replies reflect their playbook.
- **Stage 2 Drafting layer.** Human-approved drafts inside their existing inbox plus a daily owner report. Gate (proposal): customer-rated draft acceptance and stage accuracy, plus a renewal. Falsifier: low acceptance or material stage errors.
- **Stage 3 AI sales desk.** Stage-aware policy, supervisor console; replacing the inbox only if customers prefer the layer. Gate: renewals.

## First 90 days (13 weeks)
- W1-4: read-only prototype over Beeper Desktop API (L12); recruit 5 people with 3+ channels; collect 20 intents each. Owner: founder. Budget: assumption, model usage plus participant thank-you.
- W5-8: blind A/B with pooled picks, several raters (L3); one-week edit-time log; ask Moses and Repair.sg for 100 chats each. Commitment asked: chat export plus about an hour of grading.
- W9-13: apply the 70% gate; deliver two audits; ask one owner for a paid human-approved pilot or publish the null result.
- Evidence: blind-choice table, edit log, two graded audits, signed pilot request or documented kill.

## Buyers and demand
Owner-led service firms with WhatsApp as primary lead channel, dozens of enquiries/day, diagnosis-before-price sales, juniors unable to reproduce the owner's judgement, speed-sensitive conversion (tuition, home services, clinics, beauty). Excluded: simple FAQ and high-volume transactional shops. Warm paths: the two interviewees and their peers (L5). Procurement: owner-led, no formal procurement assumed. Commitment: chats then pilot.

## Credibility
Publish method, numbers and null results; open-source the local composer; document the data boundary (archive stays on device); design partners who grade; ask SleekFlow/respond.io about integration programmes (unchecked).

## Knowledge and team
Learn: consultative sales structure in one vertical; conversation-quality evaluation; WhatsApp Business Platform rules (L14-L16). Hires: conversation-quality grader, inbox-product advisor, later integration engineer. Co-founder profile: sales-operations depth (assumption). No sourced talent-market data.

## Capital path
Savings, then audit fees, pilot fees, EDGE Grant if eligible (L22: replaced EDG/PSG on 2026-09-30; >=30% SG/PR ownership; S$100k annual cap per blogs; unverified), angel or venture only after renewals. Not raising on the personal product alone because of L-power assessment.

## Regulatory, legal, ethical
Human sends every message (L12, L14). Business use needs a read of Meta's own Business Solution terms (L15 is a vendor reading) and PDPA counsel review (L17); processing other people's messages is a grey area not legally assessed. Recipient need not use the product; ethical exportability of profiles.

## Founder payout
Stage 0 none; Stage 1-2 service/pilot fees; Stage 3 salary/equity. Illustrative: 3-4 firms at SGD 3,000/month cover a SGD 6,000/month wage under assumed SGD 500-1,200 cost (L23, assumption).

## Precedents (limited)
Beeper acquired by Automattic (L13): messaging plumbing is acquirable. SleekFlow raised venture money (L20): the SMB inbox space funds companies. No clean service-to-product precedent found for a drafting layer; stated as a gap.

## Stop/go
Stage 0 stop: B <70% or A ~ B. Stage 1 stop: no new issue found. Stage 2 stop: low acceptance, material stage errors, no renewal. Stage 3 stop: churn, no renewals.
