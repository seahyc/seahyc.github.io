# @giacomozucco on self-hosted Beeper

The author endorses self-hosting because bridge-side decryption otherwise weakens the end-to-end-encryption assumptions of contacts across networks. The post values one application for Signal, Telegram, Slack, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and X.

Reported limitations include awkward contact addition/exchange, no calls, and missing email, Keet, SimpleX and Whitenoise bridges.

Signal: sovereignty is genuine demand, but the desired product is still judged by connection maintenance and everyday contact ergonomics—not by its license alone.
