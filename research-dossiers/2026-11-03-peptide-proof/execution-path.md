# Execution path — build evidence before instruments

3 November 2026. Every budget, calendar and quantitative gate in this file is a founder-selected planning assumption. No quotes, hires, contracts, funding applications, sampling or outreach have occurred. Sources below establish routes and precedents, not startup demand or a guaranteed regulatory timetable.

## Sequence of companies or stages

### Stage 0 · Days 1–30: qualify the job and the boundary

Founder sells a paid feasibility/quality-record review to a pharmacy with a named accepting quality professional. Retain US pharmacy counsel, a senior analytical chemist and an independent QA specialist before promising drug-quality conclusions. Select one analyte/form/matrix and one state/customer workflow that counsel can clear today. Experimental peptides under unsettled policy are not the default starting inventory. A narrower lawful adjacent peptide-quality workflow is acceptable only if buyers actually need it; do not silently treat wellness demand as demand for approved-drug testing.

Deliverables: eligibility matrix; last-three-lot workflow map; incumbent comparison; blinded sample plan; laboratory scope and raw-data checklist; service limitations; confidentiality/corrections rules; insurance terms; approved quotation template. No “safe”, “FDA-approved lab” or blanket “compliant peptide” badge.

Gate: legal and insurance path documented plus two unrelated pharmacies each willing to prepay at least $5,000 for a defined pilot. If direct qualified-lab buying already solves the job, stop or partner as a narrow consultant.

### Stage 1 · Months 2–6: evidence service, outsourced qualified analysis

Sell a case record, not test-result optimism. Customer and QA lead control representative sample selection; preserve seals, identifiers and custody; qualify each assay/matrix; separate ingredient results from finished-product and process evidence. A second laboratory handles predefined discrepant or blinded split samples. Preagree acceptance limits and investigation rules; do not test repeatedly until a pass. Pharmacist/quality unit retains release decisions.

Records connect manufacturer → received lot → selected sample → methods/standards → raw result → independent technical review → discrepancy/correction history. Provide a stable verification link and downloadable original reports; attach only the claim actually supported. Protect confidential manufacturing and patient information. No public ranking assembled from selected private successes.

Gate at day 90: at least ten completed cases, full traceability, no unresolved critical discrepancy, at least two paid follow-on orders, collected contribution at least 30%. Track failure holds as successful risk detection. Measure turnaround by assay scope; do not promise a chemistry-speed result for a growth-based sterility program. Ten cases are a workflow screen, not safety validation.

Precedent: Finnrick coordinates testing without operating a laboratory; its private terms describe this boundary. The entrant's added technical review, buyer governance and qualified-supplier evidence must earn their fee rather than merely copying a portal. https://www.finnrick.com/legal/partner-service-terms .

### Stage 2 · Months 6–18: repeat workflow, then decide whether to own a lab

Standardize the same buyer job across several independent pharmacies. Charge separately for supplier qualification, new-method work and extraordinary investigations so “per-lot” prices do not hide bespoke consulting. Own custody software, qualification knowledge and contractual data rights; avoid financing instruments as a substitute for demand.

An owned lab is conditional. Obtain equipment/service, premises, analytical-reference, staffing, waste, quality-system, validation, proficiency-testing and insurance bids. Commission an accreditor gap assessment for the specific scope, then apply, undergo assessment, correct deficiencies and wait for actual grant. For applicable drug-quality work, build the corresponding CGMP controls and quality agreements as well. Accreditation is not a generic product approval. A2LA route: https://a2la.org/faqs-iso-iec-170252017-accreditation-for-testing-calibration-laboratories/ . FDA outsourcing responsibilities: https://www.fda.gov/media/86193/download .

Capex gate: six months of paid repeat volume; at least three unrelated renewing buyers; written demand commitments for at least 60 cases/month; retained external backup; and a bid-based downside model that covers all-in fixed expense with no more than 60% of installed practical capacity occupied. The 60-case and 60% thresholds are proposed hurdles, not measured capacity. Decline equipment purchase if outsourcing remains better. The illustrative service model does not establish owned-lab feasibility.

### Stage 3 · Months 12–24: optional channels, separate decisions

Option A: remain an evidence company, expand the qualified-method/supplier network and sell buyer diligence to clinics. Option B: consumer sample testing, with its own willingness-to-pay, acquisition and privacy experiment. Require a prepaid consumer cohort whose direct contribution covers acquisition before expansion; free test requests do not pass.

Option C: compliant verified supply. This is a new business with licensing, import/wholesale, storage, dispensing, product-liability and conflicts analysis. Do not use a testing subsidiary as a blanket endorsement of inventory. Prefer independent external assessors and disclosed separation, or keep supply entirely outside the company. No supply launch until counsel validates the actual entity, products/forms/routes, states, customer transfers and distribution chain. 503B transfer restrictions especially prevent assuming a simple reseller storefront. Source: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/fdc-act-provisions-apply-human-drug-compounding . Default decision remains no product sales.

## Path to buyers and paid demand

Start with pharmacy owners, quality directors and pharmacists responsible for ingredient qualification. Use APC's dated member directory to identify prospects; qualify today's entity and authority before contact. Examples listed there include Belmar, Empower, Hallandale and Harbor. These are leads, not endorsements or design partners. https://a4pc.org/hubfs/PDFs/2025-7-24-%20PFM%20Alpha%20Order%20List%20-%20Current.pdf?hsLang=en .

The founder asks a mutual industry adviser for introductions, participates in relevant owner/quality sessions, and offers a record review followed by a prepaid pilot. APC lists Owner Summit and EduCon channels: https://a4pc.org/events . Do not buy an expensive booth before learning who owns the budget. No private network access is claimed. All future interpersonal outreach requires the user's separate approval of the exact first message and destination.

Ask for a commitment with a named accepting pharmacist, actual records and sampling authority, prepaid fee, permitted methods, failure reporting, data rights and an explicitly priced renewal. Ask clinics who pays and who can act on the report. Interview buyers who decline; unwillingness to buy is evidence, not a marketing obstacle to explain away.

## Path to credibility and conflict control

Publish a narrowly worded methods/limitations page and a redacted worked example with permission. Disclose who paid, who selected material and which lab performed each method. Publish a corrections policy; separate sales from technical sign-off; forbid supplier commissions/equity incentives and result-dependent pay. Obtain independent scientific advice with disclosed conflicts. Blind splits, appropriate references and a proficiency-testing plan build evidence of competence; sample count alone does not prove reliability. Seek relevant laboratory accreditation only for the operation and scope actually assessed.

PharmLabs' posted scope supplies a useful precedent: inspect analytes, methods and matrices rather than its logo. Its public schedule does not establish current issuer status or every offered test. https://lobby.pharmlabs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/PharmLabs-L25-933-R1.pdf .

## Team and knowledge to acquire

Founding combination: a commercial/technical operator plus a peptide analytical scientist experienced in method development and an empowered QA professional familiar with drug-quality records. Retain US pharmacy regulatory counsel and a pharmacist adviser; contract microbiology expertise rather than asking an LC-MS specialist to certify sterile processes. Recruit through analytical chemistry/pharmaceutical quality networks, pharmacy associations and experienced contract labs; these are proposed routes, not secured candidates.

Founder must learn chemical form/sequence, standards, HPLC/MS limits, assay vs biological activity, impurity and degradation work, sampling uncertainty, sterility vs microbial enumeration, endotoxin suitability, quality agreements, data integrity, federal/state routes and product claims. The founder need not personally run instruments but must know which claim cannot follow from a result.

## Capital path and personal outcome

Stage 0: capped founder/angel risk capital funds the $80,000 discovery envelope. Stage 1: customer prepayments and service receipts fund the next cases; avoid reliance on supplier marketing revenue. Stage 2: retained service profit or specialist angel equity can fund a bid-based lab plan only after commitments. Venture capital is optional and requires evidence of scalable reusable value beyond consulting. Equipment debt follows stable collections and contracted utilization; it does not solve unproven demand. No grant award, eligibility, nationality condition, foreign-ownership permission or investor interest has been verified; the model assumes none. A Singapore founder needs separate US entity, employment and immigration advice where relevant.

Founder compensation: the initial $15,000 three-month allowance is runway funded by risk capital, not profit. In the base operating scenario, fixed expense includes a hypothetical $6,000/month founder salary; its small residual is before tax and financing and should fund reserves before dividends. Later dividends require distributable cash and lawful corporate treatment. No acquisition, valuation, secondary or exit proceeds assumed. Selling the lab to a strategic buyer is a possible outcome, not the investment case; a profitable independent service is a legitimate end state.

## Gates that change the plan

Stop immediately on unauthorized therapeutic claims, missing lawful scope, unacceptable insurance exclusions, falsified custody or pressure to suppress an adverse result. Hold individual cases with unresolved critical discrepancies. Stop the company thesis if no two buyers renew or if full variable contribution remains below 30% at a feasible price. Stop lab ownership without downside capacity coverage and contractual demand. Stop supply-channel expansion if independent evidence and sales incentives cannot be credibly separated. Recheck policy at every customer onboarding and material route change; current draft status is a product dependency, not a footnote.
