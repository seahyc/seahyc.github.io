## Source notes: what proof needs to mean

### Chemistry — E19/E20

https://www.agilent.com/cs/library/brochures/ebook-lcgc-peptide-5994-7768en-agilent.pdf

https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/q2r2-validation-analytical-procedures

The instrument-maker technical document explains separation and characterization techniques; it is sponsored technical material, not an inter-laboratory comparison. FDA's final validation framework is the better reference for what must be demonstrated about a procedure. Proposed report fields: specific molecular form, method ID/version, reference standard, specificity, accuracy/precision, relevant limits, matrix, raw chromatograms/spectra, deviations and reviewer. Not every field is a universal legal requirement; the qualified technical lead defines fit-for-purpose scope.

HPLC separates detectable components. The area of one peak relative to others is method-dependent purity, not all material in the vial. Quantification needs calibrated comparison. Mass spectrometry adds mass information; mass agreement alone cannot settle every sequence/isomer/stereochemistry issue. Orthogonal testing and impurity characterization must match the molecule and decision. Quantity measured chemically is not demonstrated biological activity.

### Sterility, endotoxin and practice — E21–E24

https://www.usp.org/compounding/general-chapter-797

https://pages.usp.org/rs/711-SXF-172/images/USP_HQS_797_FAQs_December2023_V6__002.pdf?version=0

https://doi.usp.org/USPNF/USPNF_M12457_03_01.html

https://www.fda.gov/media/83477/download

USP public material separates preparation practices, sampled sterility testing and sterility assurance. The public <1071> preview establishes the conventional incubation constraint. FDA's March 2026 guidance covers endotoxin-method suitability and acceptance-limit justification. Killing or failing to grow microbes does not answer every contamination question. Ingredient testing cannot establish what happens after formulation, filling, transport or opening. A qualified pharmacy must decide its ingredient, finished-product, process and stability program. Do not transfer consumer EU/vial thresholds to clinical release.

### Accreditation and quality systems — E12/E13/E31

https://www.iso.org/standard/66912.html

https://a2la.org/faqs-iso-iec-170252017-accreditation-for-testing-calibration-laboratories/

https://www.fda.gov/media/86193/download

ISO public description establishes competence and impartiality as objectives. Accreditation applies to an assessed scope. A testing coordinator cannot call itself an accredited laboratory merely because a subcontractor is accredited. A2LA provides an application/assessment route, not a promised cost or schedule. FDA quality-agreement guidance preserves applicable responsibilities across outsourcing. A lab serving drug-quality decisions needs the relevant quality discipline, not only a certificate logo. Full ISO standard and current USP compendium were not licensed/read; get authorized copies for operating design.

## Buyer path, competition and what remains unknown

First economic buyer hypothesis: compounding-pharmacy owner or quality leader; accepting authority: pharmacist/quality unit with counsel. Initial tasks: ingredient-source qualification, lot discrepancies, scope gaps and audit-ready records. Telehealth clinic medical/operations leads could pay for independent pharmacy/vendor diligence, but cannot turn a coordinator into a dispenser. Consumer public transparency is a separate payer experiment.

Named lead pool, not validated customers: Belmar, Empower, Hallandale and Harbor appear in APC's dated member list. Membership establishes neither peptide activity nor suitability. Recheck entity, licenses and scope before contact. APC's Owner Summit and EduCon are plausible channels; no claimed access or booking. Sources: https://a4pc.org/hubfs/PDFs/2025-7-24-%20PFM%20Alpha%20Order%20List%20-%20Current.pdf?hsLang=en and https://a4pc.org/events .

Substitutes: existing pharmacy quality staff; direct qualified laboratory contracts; supplier COAs plus audits; Finnrick coordination; abstention from disputed compounds. Compare those before claiming a moat. A custody/data workflow and transparent failure handling could help, but reusable proprietary advantage is unproven. Avoid an invented national vial count, prevalence, market size, price premium or conversion rate.
