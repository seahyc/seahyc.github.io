## Interpretation rules

A sample can pass its specified methods while a different vial, future lot, clinical use or legal route remains unacceptable. HPLC area purity is not amount per vial, microbial absence, absence of endotoxin, clinical efficacy or an import permission. The assay/potency term in vendor menus can mean chemical quantity; do not equate it to a functional biological assay.

No total addressable market is estimated. No buyer has been contacted, no insurance quoted and no laboratory commissioned. All pilot counts, prices, budgets, stop/go thresholds and cash outcomes outside vendor menus are founder assumptions. No private or paywalled content is inferred.
