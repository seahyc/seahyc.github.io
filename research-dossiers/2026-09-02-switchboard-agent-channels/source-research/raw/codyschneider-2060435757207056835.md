## Paraphrase

The product request calls for a production service that accepts targets, message/template, and campaign settings; performs conservative first-touch Instagram outreach; tracks delivery and replies; and exposes callbacks, API, webhooks, or exports. The vendor must own sender setup, limits, health, recovery, client separation, and platform-change maintenance. One-off scripts and fragile self-hosted repos do not qualify for the bounty.

## Requested ideal surface

The brief describes an experience equivalent to `POST /campaigns`, followed by asynchronous target states and reply/status callbacks.
