## Paraphrase

Anuj objects that using Unipile for one narrow function across 50 accounts costs $275 monthly. He argues that per-account access to a broad integration bundle is a poor fit when the application needs only one operation.

## Interpretation

A vertical product can price around useful campaign capacity or usage rather than charging for every connected account and unused platform capability.
