# Marketplace project scan — 2026-09-03

Sources inspected: live Upwork search/result pages in Dia, plus public Freelancer and PeoplePerHour listings.

## Verdict

Projects exist in meaningful numbers, but they split into two different markets:

1. **Agent/automation builds:** buyers accept software and pay for qualification, routing, resumption, CRM integration, deterministic actions, reliability, and ongoing support.
2. **Creator relationship labor:** buyers pay humans to research, message, follow up, remember context, and sound natural; several explicitly reject mass automation or AI-heavy messaging.

The first is the easiest near-term revenue market for Switchboard. The second validates the relationship-aware voice problem but not autonomous sending. For creator outreach, the initial product should be human-approved and openly positioned as managed execution, not a hidden bot replacing a relationship manager.

## What this changes

- Marketplace projects can finance discovery and generate production evidence before a SaaS build.
- The best initial service offer is broader than cold creator blasting: **build or operate an agent-owned messaging workflow that never loses a conversation and preserves the sender's voice.**
- Instagram creator outreach remains a credible vertical demo, but human approval and clear automation disclosure should be part of the initial product.
- WhatsApp/inbound qualification has more obvious implementation demand and lower unofficial-access risk; it is a strong revenue bridge while Instagram cold outreach is hardened.
- Marketplace search-result counts are highly contaminated by generic marketing work. Individual project scopes and costly behavior are the reliable signal.

## Recommended acquisition test

Apply selectively to three projects, each testing a different hypothesis:

1. Instagram qualification bot — tests product/architecture fit.
2. UGC Instagram campaign — tests whether managed, human-approved execution can replace labor.
3. AI automation agency partner — tests distribution through implementation partners.

Use one working Switchboard prototype/Loom and a tailored two-paragraph proposal for each. Do not use generic bidding or hide the automation from a buyer requesting manual work.
