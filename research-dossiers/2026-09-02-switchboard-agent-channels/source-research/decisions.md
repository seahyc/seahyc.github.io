# Decisions

## Current gate

G2 Shape: **conditional pass**.

The evidence clears language, behavior, and initial economics: a named buyer has published the exact job, rejected self-hosted substitutes, attached a $5K bounty, and promised recurring spend. Adjacent products demonstrate both budget and active supply.

It does not clear a broad platform-build gate. DMnode already mirrors the brief, Unipile exposes the cross-channel primitives, and unofficial platform access creates real account and maintenance risk.

## Build boundary

Build only the smallest Instagram campaign runtime around Switchboard's existing connector. Do not build native LinkedIn, a general omnichannel dashboard, a sender marketplace, or a full SleekFlow replacement before a paid pilot.

## Primary assumption to test

Customers pay Switchboard to own campaign and sender continuity, while retaining control of the business identity and relationship data.

## Secondary assumption to test

Per-person/per-channel voice plus cross-channel relationship memory increases positive replies enough to differentiate Switchboard from transport APIs and bulk-DM tools.

## Kill criterion

If qualified teams want only an open-source repo, refuse to connect real accounts, or will not pay an ongoing managed fee after a successful monitored campaign, keep Switchboard as personal/open-source infrastructure and stop the hosted outreach product.
