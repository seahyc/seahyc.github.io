# Make It Hit decision log

## 2026-09-02 — Narrow from multichannel platform to Instagram pilot

- Decision: run a capped Instagram creator-outreach test; defer native LinkedIn and a broad SMB inbox.
- Gate: G2 Shape.
- Verdict: conditional pass.
- Evidence: Cody bounty/specification, existing Switchboard Instagram implementation, public pricing, direct competitor activity, and first-party operational complaints.
- Counterevidence: only one explicit bounty buyer; DMnode already mirrors the brief; Unipile covers transport; no Switchboard campaign, payment, activation, or retention evidence.
- Reversal condition: if every qualified pilot requires native LinkedIn, reconsider a provider adapter before native development. If customers refuse real-account connection or recurring managed payment, stop the hosted product.

## 2026-09-02 — Make relationship-aware voice the experience differentiator

- Decision: demonstrate per-person/per-channel tone in the first pilot, but do not claim conversion uplift before testing.
- Reason: it maps to YC's direct pain and uses Switchboard's people graph; transport-only positioning is easily copied.
- Reversal condition: if it creates high approval burden or no observable reply-quality benefit, demote voice to an optional customer-controlled layer.

## 2026-09-02 — Open data plane, paid operations

- Decision: preserve an inspectable/self-hostable Switchboard core while testing willingness to pay for managed sender and campaign continuity.
- Reason: aligns with user preference and reduces identity/data lock-in while preserving a commercial job Cody explicitly wants delegated.
- Reversal condition: if protocol publication materially accelerates breakage or abuse, keep fragile recovery adapters service-operated while retaining portable schemas, history, and revocation.

## 2026-09-03 — Use marketplace service projects as the first acquisition test

- Decision: test three project types before broad SaaS acquisition: an Instagram qualification build, a managed UGC campaign, and a white-label automation-agency partnership.
- Gate: G3 Package / G4 Simulate.
- Evidence: current Upwork listings pay for the exact component jobs and repeatedly demand personalization, resumption, deterministic actions, CRM state, reliability, and ongoing support.
- Counterevidence: creator-relationship buyers frequently prefer manual humans and sometimes explicitly ban automation; marketplace competition is heavy and lower-end budgets are commoditized.
- Product change: keep human approval in the creator-outreach pilot and sell an outcome/implementation first, not seats or raw API access.
- Reversal condition: if tailored applications with a working Switchboard demonstration produce no qualified conversation, revisit the offer and buyer before adding product scope.
