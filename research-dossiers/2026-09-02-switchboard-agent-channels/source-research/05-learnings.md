# Learnings

## Established enough to act on

- The requested job is not generic messaging access; it is reliable operational ownership of proactive creator outreach that plugs into the customer's agents.
- Switchboard's existing Instagram implementation substantially reduces prototype cost.
- Official Instagram messaging cannot satisfy arbitrary cold first-touch outreach, creating both the opening and the principal risk.
- The campaign API/feature surface is highly copyable; DMnode's rapid appearance demonstrates this.
- The strongest visible demo is a believable creator conversation completed by the customer's agent, not a transport or audit dashboard.
- Current marketplace buyers independently specify conversation resumption, deterministic state transitions, human handoff, natural personal tone, and ongoing maintenance—the same capabilities implied by Switchboard's architecture.
- Creator outreach is already funded as recurring labor, including provided lists, personalized messages, follow-ups, qualification, and spreadsheet tracking.

## Still hypotheses

- Relationship-aware voice will outperform lighter template personalization.
- User-owned trust roots affect purchase or account-connection confidence.
- A $499 managed workspace supports delivery and acquisition economics.
- UGC-heavy B2C teams have recurring enough campaigns for retention.
- Open-source technical distribution will reach buyers rather than only non-paying builders.

## Contradictions to preserve

- Cody explicitly rejects self-hosting, while YC personally prefers open-source/self-written tools. The product must separate customer-owned data/control from paid operational ownership rather than assume one preference applies to the entire segment.
- Broad cross-channel capability is strategically attractive, but every unofficial channel adds a distinct reliability and policy stack. Breadth can weaken the wedge before it becomes an advantage.
- Personalization is desirable, but additional research and approval steps can erase the speed advantage if poorly designed.
- Several creator-relationship buyers explicitly reject bots or AI-heavy messaging. This supports assistive memory and human approval while weakening a fully autonomous replacement claim.
