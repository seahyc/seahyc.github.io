## Founder decision
Should the founder (Switchboard's owner) build a managed Instagram creator-outreach runtime for customers' agents (customer: growth-agent builders and UGC-heavy B2C teams), starting with a capped concierge pilot (wedge), before any native LinkedIn or broad omnichannel product?

## Desired reader conclusion
Conditional pass for a thin Instagram pilot; no broad platform; evidence is one buyer plus structural reasoning, so the pilot exists to find out whether anyone pays for operational ownership.

## Core assumptions
1. Customers pay for sender and campaign continuity (primary).
2. Relationship-aware voice differentiates (secondary, untested).
3. Instagram is a sufficient wedge without native LinkedIn.
4. Managed fee is $200–500/month (numbered file says $499).

## Disproof
No two qualified teams connect real senders; every pilot self-hosts or wants only source; no recurring payment after a good campaign; unrecoverable account damage.
