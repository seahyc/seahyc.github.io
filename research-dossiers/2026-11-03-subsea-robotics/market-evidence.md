# Market evidence — subsea robotics

Research cutoff/access: 3 November 2026. Source bodies were opened through web research; IMDA and Finlex required signed-in Dia browser rendering. Only public pages were read. No paid research, customer outreach, private fault records, hands-on product benchmark, supplier quote or field deployment. URLs and per-claim limits are in evidence-ledger.md; this file records interpretation, disagreements and missing commercial evidence.

## 1. Buying categories, not a single underwater-robot market

| Segment | Paying decision and acceptable object — hypotheses unless a contract is cited | Procurement evidence | Main unpriced constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Telecom cable owner/consortium | Route condition survey, post-event evidence or maintenance programme | ICPC confirms industry fault/maintenance context; no new local survey award found | Access to route/baseline, survey cadence, owner acceptance and existing maintenance-zone arrangements |
| Offshore wind owner/developer | Cable corridor/burial/structure survey tied to build or O&M action | Fugro Taiwan geotechnical award is verified; not an O&M AUV award | Internal electrical defects need different tests; marine permission and local delivery |
| Offshore pipeline operator | Geophysical/visual integrity inspection to a named scope | EIVA reports commercial Equinor unmanned survey | Incumbent acceptance, sensors, full project logistics and standoff safety |
| Defence | Mission capability plus testing, integration and sustainment | DSTA BeeX contract; Australia Ghost Shark; DIU SCUUV transition | Programme sponsorship, eligibility, technical assurance, export/data access and cash timing |
| Vehicle OEM | Qualified component/ subsystem, not a paper specification | Kraken financial report establishes products/services; no founder design win | Reliability envelope, qualification cost, origin restrictions and committed order volume |

No defensible local TAM follows. Public cable landings are **stock/capacity**, not contracted survey workload. Useful capacity is the subset a specified sensor/vehicle can inspect. Available capacity additionally requires permission, time windows and recovery. Profitable capacity requires paid accepted output covering all charged days and company costs. The missing bridge is named annual eligible kilometres × actual paid cadence × feasible won share × accepted price, with no double-counting of contractor and owner's spend.

## 4. Mechanism and capability envelope

### WHOI / NOAA / NOC — E08/E09/E27
https://acomms.whoi.edu/micro-modem
https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/sound.html
https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/expedition-feature/technology-development-partnerships-ex1904-logs-july25-2/
https://www.noc.ac.uk/our-work/marine-technology/ocean-vehicles/autonomous-underwater-vehicles
WHOI's link is a specific modem example: packet rate is not net throughput, and export availability is explicitly restricted for some hardware. NOAA supplies the sound-speed basis for the propagation example; its REMUS account describes launch, GPS at the surface, INS, periodic surfacing, recovery and post-processing. NOC describes integrated underwater navigation. These sources support first-principles explanation, not a universal sensor spec, speed, autonomy safety claim or job-cost benchmark.

Proposed envelope to negotiate, not achieved specifications: shallow, permissioned corridor, known baseline, observably exposed cable/seabed features, bounded current/visibility and launch/recovery access. Required payload is selected by the observation: sonar for seabed geometry, optical evidence where visibility supports it, additional compatible sensing for buried-cable claims. Exact depth, standoff, position tolerance and critical change sizes must come from the customer's job specification. Do not invent one universal 'inspection-grade accuracy'. Exclude internal electrical health and intervention/repair from initial scope.

### Cable-following preprint — E15
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23606
The paper text and abstract report a partial visual field result on a short test cable, including reacquisition after loss. This is not a modern fleet performance estimate. It is useful because it makes visible the denominator omitted by robot-motion videos: continuous accepted coverage can be lost even when the vehicle keeps moving. Local evaluation must include unobserved sections and positional uncertainty.

## 8. What the headline omits

A telecom cable, power cable and pipeline are different inspection objects. Inspecting external geometry does not diagnose internal cable insulation or stop the next anchor. A photograph of an AUV omits launch/recovery crew, vessel days, post-processing, unobserved areas, and customer acceptance. A contract ceiling omits exercised options and sustainment scope. A component's gross margin omits qualification effort and central costs. A recurring service story omits actual cadence, bidder concentration, contractor share, data rights and customer collections.

No public evidence obtained here resolves vessel charter terms, weather-charged days, deductible/liability, failure frequency, committed annual eligible survey length, actual bid prices, current owner survey budgets, accepted positional standards, export classification or willingness to switch. These are the paid-research/data-room questions an investor should ask next. No assumed 'frontier labs won't build this' moat: incumbents, customers and vehicle OEMs are the immediate competitors; physical integration is necessary but not sufficient differentiation.
