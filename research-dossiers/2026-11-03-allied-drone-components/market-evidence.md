# Market evidence — allied drone components

Research date: 3 November 2026. Phase 1. Search and direct primary-page reading, plus Dia where fetching blocked or hid rendered data. No Gemini research requested, used or submitted. A supplier researcher wrote supplier-notes.md; the lead independently reopened the Scorpion, APD, Plettenberg company and PDW pages and reruns the scenario calculations. No supplier/customer outreach or hands-on hardware tests occurred.

## Decision-changing findings

The evidence supports the importance of traceability and the existence of a differentiated procurement problem. It does **not** identify a category with literally no affordable non-Chinese source. The single-component candidate is a buyer-specific high-voltage ESC, conditionally selected for tractable contracted manufacture and measurable acceptance. This is a working hypothesis; another source of a satisfactory component is a successful discovery result even if it kills the new-hardware company.

There are two different products hidden in “sell proof”: independent conformity assessment, and a component maker's version-controlled evidence package. DIU already recognizes third-party assessment organizations. The proposed company must deliver a repeatable physical SKU with evidence usable by those assessors and OEMs; it should not assume a right to issue official approvals.

## Primary regulation and procurement notes

### China Announcement 49: precise controls, not a universal motor ban

[MOFCOM original](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zc/art/2026/art_b2fa4e60bcd24bdf8c658e476b808785.html), issued 19 October; effective 18 November 2026. Read the Chinese original. The motor provision is conjunctive, including power density and rated speed, with a design-purpose condition. The ESC definition is linked to the controlled motor definition. It also addresses autopilots, specified communications, propellers and technology. Announcement contains no named destination ban. This proves classification/licensing work is necessary; it does not establish shipment stoppage or the next marginal order's approval probability. Ledger E01/E02 retains exact thresholds.

### FCC: two distinctions that can overturn the Asia-first US thesis

[FCC expanded FAQ](https://www.fcc.gov/covered-list-faqs-uas-and-uas-critical-components), updated 21 July 2026. Browser fetch initially required Dia; Expand All exposed the full public body, saved in fcc-faq-dia.txt. Foreign production is the relevant framing, not merely corporate nationality or Chinese ownership. Existing authorized models and federal-exclusive use are treated differently from new equipment authorization. Components that previously did not require authorization do not newly require it. The waiver onshoring language can still reach those components.

The two Blue-list expiry answers conflict, 2028 versus 2027. Neither should be silently chosen. Framework components and Cleared platforms are different programme concepts; do not promote a supplier's Framework listing into automatic FCC exemption. The [DA25-1086 notice](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-25-1086A1.pdf) is now read through parent-extracted fcc-da-25-1086.txt. Its determination says “UAS and UAS critical components must be produced in the United States.” Listing is by production location; DoW/DHS specific determinations can remove an item/class; the operative restriction is FCC equipment authorization. The notice has no exemption expiry dates and does not resolve the FAQ conflict. The FAQ describes Buy American domestic-end-product treatment under 48 CFR 25.101(a) and Conditional Approvals. ESCs are not expressly named in the non-exhaustive list; the primarily-for-UAS definition and existing authorization exemptions require exact-SKU classification. Neither coverage nor an authorization requirement is assumed for this proposed ESC.

### Assessment: proof is already a purchased service

[DIU Recognized Assessors](https://www.diu.mil/latest/diu-seeking-recognized-assessors-to-support-blue-uas-ndaa-compliance), 19 September 2025. Direct web fetching intermittently timed out; Dia body read and saved. Organizations named are AUVSI, Dark Wolf, Edgesource, Legion X, MTSI and SpiderOak. Suppliers can receive proposed costs and timelines after portal submission. DIU/government retains the certification decision. Country-of-origin, ownership, sub-component sourcing and firmware/software/hardware artifacts are explicitly in scope. The value proposition is real but not an empty category; assessor fees and elapsed times were not published in the body.

[DIU refresh announcement](https://www.diu.mil/latest/blue-uas-refresh-list-and-framework-platforms-and-capabilities-selected), February 2025 with March updates, distinguishes selected-for-verification from completed additions. It documents Framework components and platform approval separately. Do not use its 2025 vendor list as an exhaustive November 2026 supplier database.

### EO and actual aircraft demand: policy, orders, delivery and payment differ

[EO14307](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/06/unleashing-american-drone-dominance/), 6 June 2025, is a primary domestic-production policy signal, not an Asian-component purchasing waiver. [Army's 3 December 2025 account](https://www.army.mil/article/289322/war_department_asks_industry_to_make_more_than_300k_drones_quickly_cheaply) describes a USD1bn programme and approximately 340,000 small UAS over two years, with changing phase quantities/prices. [Current DDP page](https://dronedominance.mil/index.html) describes intent to buy over 200,000 by 2027 and first-phase 30,000 orders. These are differing programme snapshots; do not add them or treat them as delivered inventory. This programme includes aircraft types that may not use the proposed high-voltage ESC at all.

[Official leaderboard](https://dronedominance.mil/leaderboard.html) is JS-heavy; Dia selected GAUNTLET I, captured gauntlet-i-dia.txt and closed the tab. Data says current 2 November 2026 at 15:08 EDT. Original rows are fully accepted. Summation: original orders/accepted = 22,320; bonus orders = 7,680; combined = 30,000. Accepted bonus is 1,520; 480 pending; 5,680 not shipped. Total accepted = 23,840. These are arithmetic reconstructions of the visible rows, not programme cash receipts. The page also retains generic prototype-finalist language; do not imply that every future award is guaranteed. This is much better evidence for the dated fulfillment state than a present-tense repetition of September reporting.

### Taiwan: established direction, uncertain addressable releases

[Executive Yuan policy](https://www.ey.gov.tw/Page/5A8A0CB5B41DA11E/97980e62-02ab-4c1f-b727-c51233e2ffc2), 7 May 2026, reports component localization, testing infrastructure and international links, as well as historical 2024 procurement. [AIDC's November 2024 presentation](https://www.aidc.com.tw/Content/File/20241114_conference_tw.pdf) states the older 15,000/month by 2028 target: 180,000/year is a multiplication, not production. The public presentation prohibits copying its graphics without permission; link, do not reuse its imagery.

No attempt is made to reconcile conflicting Taiwan future aircraft procurement totals into one market size. Latest tenders, award quantities, delivery years, programme definitions, OEM BOMs and accepted sub-tier countries are needed. Even an accurate aircraft target is not a startup ESC release forecast. Taiwan may be a practical source of manufacturing partners; policy pages do not establish access, price or willingness to accept a new supplier.

### Import and export work belong in economics

[CBP CSMS69738151](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/bulletins/4281ea7), 2 September 2026, describes different UAS-related duty provisions and future component treatment; some country headings were explicitly pending further guidance. The briefing is a dated source, not assurance of the correct November entry treatment for an unclassified ESC. Ask the actual importer to verify current HTS, end use, exclusions, origin and date. Do not apply a blanket allied 15% assumption.

[BIS classification guidance](https://www.bis.gov/licensing/classify-your-item) makes commodities, software and technology relevant; HTS and ECCN are different. [August 2026 drone export rule](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-08-14/pdf/2026-16628.pdf) is further evidence that controls change. Do not assign ITAR/EAR jurisdiction from the word “drone.” An Asia-based team can create US-person/data-transfer issues depending on actual design/data; counsel must classify the real transaction. No proposed product has been classified in this research.

The founder's possible advantage is the shortest path from a documented failed procurement to an accepted, repeatable second source. Its proposed assets are rights to a portable design/test package, lot genealogy, controlled firmware/BOM changes, a dependable CM and repeated buyer acceptance. These are requirements for an experiment, not a moat already earned. If all valuable work is custom NRE and buyers do not reorder, call it an engineering service.

## Counter-drone layer and lawful buyer scope

Detect → track → identify → decide → defeat is a conceptual workflow. It separates sensing/association/classification from authority and effect. [FAA airport guidance](https://www.faa.gov/airports/new_entrants/uas_detection_mitigation_response) notes that detection does not establish intent. Radar, RF, optical/thermal and acoustic observations have different limitations; no range, accuracy or all-weather performance is inferred here.

Civilian detection buyers and authorized mitigation integrators are distinct. [FBI April 2026 testimony](https://www.fbi.gov/news/speeches-and-testimony/statement-of-special-agent-in-charge-douglas-olson-to-senate-appropriations) describes newer authority for qualified law enforcement; [July rule](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-07-06/pdf/2026-13609.pdf) addresses those agencies. An older statement that only four federal departments may act is therefore insufficient as a current universal US claim. Certain sensing itself can implicate communications/surveillance laws.

[Australia's ACMA guidance](https://www.acma.gov.au/jammers-are-illegal-australia), updated July 2026, prohibits ordinary drone-jammer possession/supply/use. These examples support careful market segmentation. They do not verify the phrase “civil buyers may only detect in most countries”: no cross-country legal census was performed. Do not generalize US or Australian rules to Singapore, Taiwan or Europe. Authorized interceptor OEMs might need propulsion components; a site buying detection does not create demand for an ESC.

## What the headline omits

The scarce object may be an accepted lot at a delivery date, not a missing motor design. Qualification is per revision and operating envelope. Origin can change without an exterior part changing. Somebody has to fund destructive samples, rejected units, assessor work, warranty claims and working capital. A supplier's maximum rating can assume airflow the customer's installation lacks. A non-Chinese corporate logo does not remove upstream dependencies. NRE acceptance and hardware release timing can diverge. Government budget scale says little about the particular customer's unit allowance or legal eligibility.

The scenario model therefore separates attempt yield, accepted-unit cost, NRE projects, annual fixed costs, tooling, cash timing and duty shock. It does not use TAM/SAM/SOM. The next data to buy are two matched incumbent/CM quotes, an agreed acceptance plan, a lot-level provenance package, and two paying customers. All counts, timeframes and stop/go thresholds in research-map.md are proposed founder rules.
