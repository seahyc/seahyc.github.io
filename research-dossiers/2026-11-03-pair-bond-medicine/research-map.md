# Pair-bond medicine: research map

Research date: 3 November 2026. Phase 1 only. Public evidence and explicit planning assumptions; no customers contacted, prescribing performed, or clinical experiment conducted.

## Founder decision

Should a founder build a relationship-care company for consenting adult couples who want help communicating, starting with a clinician-referred, measured, non-drug skills programme, rather than a product that promises to chemically preserve a marriage?

Working answer: fund a small disconfirmation exercise for route (d), not an oxytocin marriage-drug company. Venture scale is an unproved subsequent gate. A useful small company and a venture-scale drug developer are distinct outcomes. US-first is a proposed commercial scope, not an assumption that Singapore founders have US clinical or grant eligibility.

Desired reader conclusion: the human ambition deserves attention, but the intervention must improve freely chosen well-being, not maximize marital persistence. Drug discovery, licensed clinical practice, infrastructure and consumer support have different evidence requirements, buyers and capital structures. A financing precedent for depression medicine is not proof of a divorce-prevention market.

## Core assumptions to test

- Adults voluntarily seek structured help before crisis; both partners independently opt in.
- A specific referral/engagement problem remains after comparison with OurRelationship, Paired, ordinary couples therapy and doing nothing.
- A narrow product can improve completion or access without claiming an unproved clinical effect.
- A referring practice or sponsoring benefits intermediary pays enough to cover human support, safety work and distribution.
- Outcome measurement can be collected without exposing private partner answers to the other partner, employer or an unsafe household.
- An initial service can become repeatable; clinical efficacy, distribution efficiency and company value do not follow from engagement alone.

## Strongest bear case

There is no investable new category here yet. Oxytocin's compelling animal story does not establish a reproducible human treatment. Acute social effects need not create durable affection, and more attachment can be harmful in an abusive relationship. Relationship distress is discussed as a clinical focus category; full current DSM text remains unread, and no ready-made bonding-drug indication is established (E23). Entactogen-assisted therapy faces drug-development, safety, blinding and delivery questions. Pharma's selected clinical-asset acquisitions do not establish acquisition odds for an early relationship asset. The legal non-drug business is crowded and cheap; the proposed wrapper could be an ordinary service with extra measurement costs. A clinician may prefer an existing evidence-based program or simply a PDF and follow-up call. No customer evidence gathered here defeats this case.

## Route selection and falsifiers

Compare all four routes in market-evidence.md; route (d) is selected only for a capped validation stage. Do not quietly convert this into an oxytocin telehealth funnel. Routes (a) and (c) can be revisited only with indication-specific human evidence, lawful access, specialist team, protectable value and independent financing.

Proposed gates, not empirical benchmarks: by day 30, at least 2 of 3 candidate practices name the same problem and identify a budget owner after seeing incumbent alternatives; by day 60, 3 practices sign a paid scope at the model price or explain a different viable price; by day 90, two renew commercially. Completed-course support must be at most 1 hour per enrolled couple at the base cost assumption; for late entrants that gate and final outcomes fall around day 120–150, not day 90. Measure offer-to-two-partner-consent, activation, completion, missing outcomes, safety escalations and refund rate. A small pilot can reject poor usability/economics; it cannot prove efficacy or safety.

Stop immediately for a consent/privacy design failure or inability to staff a clinically responsible escalation route; pause the affected workflow for any serious safety concern. Stop the company thesis if buyers prefer an acceptable incumbent or if no margin remains after support and acquisition. Do not declare success because couples stayed married.

## Gate 1 corrections applied · 3 November 2026

1. **No TAM/distress denominator.** This dossier establishes neither a couples-distress population nor a lifetime-divorce denominator. Divorce events are context only, never an addressable market or demand estimate.
2. **FDA letter scope.** Public prose will use the confirmed reliability-of-safety-data finding from the 8 August 2024 CRL (https://download.open.fda.gov/crl/CRL_NDA215455_20240808.pdf). Broader durability/expectancy descriptions in research notes are not public headline claims without section-specific attribution.
3. **Learning service, not self-financing.** Month-12 target of 100 paid enrolments creates only $12,905 contribution before fixed costs in the assumed base model; annual break-even is 2,325 enrolments. The pilot cannot self-finance this company or a drug programme.
4. **Price and deal provenance.** OurRelationship $249/$99/$79 menus were collected by the research agent, not re-verified by the parent cross-model review. They are dated observations, not checkout guarantees. Lilly's $4.2bn combined IPR&D item is not the AtaiBeckley deal price; the filed deal is approximately $2.8bn upfront plus up to $1bn contingent.
