# Regulatory and transaction verification

Research date: 2026-11-03. Primary regulator, government and issuer sources below were opened and read with the web tool; search snippets alone were not treated as proof. This is a research map, not legal advice or permission to administer a drug. No git commands, browser actions, or other repository edits were made for this slice.

## Decision-relevant findings

- US compounding access, controlled-substance research, patient access and marketing approval are different routes. None establishes an approved pair-bonding indication.
- Australia created narrow psychiatrist access to unapproved MDMA for PTSD and psilocybine for treatment-resistant depression (TRD) from July 2023. It did not legalise relationship enhancement or retail psychedelic supply.
- Singapore has clinical-trial and controlled-drug licensing machinery, but its existence is not evidence that a relationship-enhancement protocol would be permitted.
- The April 2026 US executive order and July–September 2026 Lilly transaction are real in the primary sources checked. Do not dismiss them as future-date hallucinations relative to this report's November 2026 date.
- AbbVie's $1.2 billion bretisilocin figure is a ceiling including milestones; its later SEC filing distinguishes about $900 million upfront and up to $300 million additional payments.

## US oxytocin and compounding

### R1 — Pitocin prescription indications: verified, high confidence

Source: FDA-hosted Pitocin label, digitally signed 2021-05-11, accessed 2026-11-03: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/018261Orig1s041lbl.pdf

The label identifies prescription oxytocin injection for IV infusion or IM administration. Obstetric indications include medically indicated labor induction, selected uterine inertia, adjunctive management of incomplete/inevitable abortion, and control of postpartum uterine bleeding. It explicitly excludes elective induction without a medical indication. This proves a real approved oxytocin product and obstetric uses; it does not establish nasal formulation equivalence, CNS efficacy, relationship treatment, or a safe general-use bonding product. The PDF warns it may not be the latest label; verify a chosen marketed product's current label before operational decisions.

Historical caveat: Retrophin's 2014 SEC filing says Syntocinon nasal spray was FDA-approved for milk let-down and voluntarily withdrawn from US sale in 1997 for commercial reasons. Read: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1438533/000157104914000062/t1400027-s1a.htm (Syntocinon section). This primary issuer account prevents the overstatement “nasal oxytocin was never FDA-approved.” It does not establish current marketed approval or a relationship indication. Exact historical FDA withdrawal notice and current Orange Book record were not independently opened in this slice.

### R2 — Compounded nasal oxytocin is not an FDA-approved relationship medicine: verified framework, qualified product conclusion

FDA compounding Q&A, accessed 2026-11-03: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/compounding-and-fda-questions-and-answers

FDA states compounded drugs do not receive FDA approval or premarket verification of safety, effectiveness and quality. It differentiates 503A pharmacies/physicians from registered 503B outsourcing facilities; the latter face CGMP. Prescription promotion must be accurate and nonmisleading. Inference: a compounded nasal oxytocin prescription cannot truthfully be described as an FDA-approved pair-bonding treatment merely because oxytocin injection has approval. Do not call a compounded preparation an “FDA-approved drug used off-label”; distinguish the unapproved compounded formulation from off-label prescribing of an approved finished drug.

### R3 — 503A bulk eligibility: verified rule; case-specific compliance unresolved

FDA rule summary, accessed 2026-11-03: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/bulk-drug-substances-used-compounding-under-section-503a-fdc-act

503A bulk substances must comply with an applicable USP/NF monograph and compounding chapter; if there is no monograph they must be an approved-drug component; failing both, they need inclusion on the 503A bulks list. A valid certificate of analysis and registered manufacturer are also required. Oxytocin is an approved-drug component (R1); its absence from a nominated-substances list alone therefore would not prove ineligibility. This is not a blanket finding that every oxytocin API, salt, nasal recipe, prescriber or fulfillment model qualifies. Exact API identity/monograph, patient-specific prescription, state rules and all remaining 503A conditions need pharmacy counsel review before a launch.

### R4 — 503B bulk eligibility is materially narrower: verified, high confidence; oxytocin interim category not verified

FDA framework: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/bulk-drug-substances-used-compounding-under-section-503b-fdc-act

FDA permits bulk use where the substance is on the clinical-need bulks list or the compounded drug product appears on FDA's shortage list at compounding, distribution and dispensing. Approved-ingredient status alone does not satisfy this rule. FDA's posted final list (page dated 2023-08-21), opened 2026-11-03, does not include oxytocin: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/503b-bulk-drug-substances-list

Unresolved: no current oxytocin-specific interim enforcement category or current shortage record was verified. Do not infer that a shortage of an injectable permits unrelated nasal formulations, or that a listed outsourcing facility/product is FDA approved. Do not claim all 503B oxytocin compounding is prohibited: compounding from approved finished drugs, a relevant shortage, interim policy and product-specific facts need separate assessment. A 2024 FDA QuVa warning letter concerns compounded IV oxytocin, not permission for a nasal relationship product: https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/quva-pharma-inc-675685-01262024 (opened).

## US MDMA and 2026 policy

### R5 — Lykos 2024 CRL: verified sponsor disclosure, high confidence on event

Lykos company release dated 2024-08-09, opened 2026-11-03: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lykos-therapeutics-announces-complete-response-letter-for-midomafetamine-capsules-for-ptsd-302219182.html

Lykos reported FDA had completed its NDA review, would not approve on the submitted data, and requested another Phase 3 safety/efficacy trial. This is a CRL for adult PTSD midomafetamine capsules, not proof MDMA can never be approved or that pair therapy was considered. The release's disputes with FDA are sponsor opinion. The underlying CRL itself was not obtained; attribute its specific contents to Lykos rather than quote them as an independently read FDA letter. FDA's June 4 2024 meeting page was also opened: https://www.fda.gov/advisory-committees/advisory-committee-calendar/updated-meeting-time-and-public-participation-information-june-4-2024-meeting-psychopharmacologic

### R6 — DEA Schedule I and research: verified, high confidence

DEA current schedules page, opened 2026-11-03: https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/schedules/schedules.html explicitly lists MDMA among Schedule I examples. Research registration route: https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/drugreg/schedule-1.html (opened). Schedule I research registration is distinct from ordinary prescribing and commercial supply. Human investigation also needs the applicable FDA/ethics pathway; DEA registration alone is not FDA trial permission. This source does not resolve each state's additional requirements.

### R7 — April 18 2026 EO 14401: verified actual text, high confidence

White House actual presidential action: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/04/accelerating-medical-treatments-for-serious-mental-illness/ ; official printed text: https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/eo-14401.pdf (search discovery; HTML text was opened/read).

The order directs priority vouchers for qualifying breakthrough-designated psychedelics; FDA/DEA facilitation of eligible Right to Try access including necessary Schedule I handling authorizations; at least $50 million from existing ARPA-H funds for state partnerships; interagency research/data collaboration; and timely review for possible product-specific rescheduling following successful Phase 3 and ultimate FDA approval. It remains subject to law and appropriations. It is not blanket rescheduling, automatic approval, proof of clinical efficacy, or open access for healthy couples. FDA's April 24 implementation release was discovered but not opened in this slice: https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-accelerates-action-treatments-serious-mental-illness-following-executive-order

## Australia

### R8 — July 2023 access and actual controls: verified, high confidence

TGA contemporaneous safeguards: https://www.tga.gov.au/news/news-articles/update-mdma-and-psilocybin-access-and-safeguards-1-july-2023 (opened). From 2023-07-01, authorised psychiatrists could use MDMA for PTSD and psilocybin for TRD within psychedelic-assisted psychotherapy; HREC approval precedes TGA Authorised Prescriber approval. Import/manufacture and state/territory controlled-drug permissions remain necessary.

Current detailed guidance (opened 2026-11-03): https://www.tga.gov.au/resources/guidance/accessing-mdma-34-methylenedioxy-methamphetamine-and-psilocybine-psychiatrist

Current controls include specialist psychiatrist registration, a clinical treatment protocol with consent information, registered HREC approval, and TGA application for each product. TGA limits this AP route to the two named indications. Product custody/oversight stays with the prescriber; direct dispensing to patients or other therapists is prohibited under relevant state/territory handling requirements, and some jurisdictions prohibit delegation of administration. Conditions and reporting apply. These are unapproved therapeutic goods access provisions, not ordinary marketing authorisation or permission for couples enrichment. Current guidance may contain later refinements; do not present every current procedural detail as necessarily present on July 1 2023.

## Singapore

### R9 — Controlled drugs and oxytocin: verified regulator sources, high confidence on narrow findings

CNB Ecstasy page (updated 2026-07-02, opened): https://www.cnb.gov.sg/singapore-drug-situation/drugs--psychoactive-substances--and-inhalants/ecstasy/ identifies MDMA and related controlled substances. CNB plants page (opened): https://www.cnb.gov.sg/singapore-drug-situation/plants-that-contain-controlled-drugs/ identifies psilocybin as controlled under the Misuse of Drugs Act. These are enforcement/public information sources, not an approval route.

Singapore government National Drug Formulary Syntocinon record, updated 2026-10-09 (opened): https://www.ndf.gov.sg/about-drugs/product-information/sin02496p/ lists oxytocin injection SIN02496P as prescription-only with obstetric indications. It proves registered injectable access, not a nasal relationship indication. No exhaustive HSA register search for nasal oxytocin or MDMA approval was completed, so avoid asserting a comprehensive negative solely from this record.

### R10 — Research route exists but is not guaranteed: verified, high confidence

HSA regulatory overview, opened 2026-11-03: https://www.hsa.gov.sg/other-regulations/clinical-trials/overview/

Applicable CTA/CTN/CTC pathways and clinical research material (CRM) controls operate alongside ethics approval. HSA requires controlled-drug/psychotropic licences in addition to applicable trial/CRM acknowledgements. Imported/manufactured CRM must be used in an IRB-approved protocol; unused CRM must generally be disposed/exported within six months after study completion, with written permission needed for other uses. No source here grants a particular protocol or a clinic the right to administer MDMA.

HSA controlled-drug licence guidance, updated 2026-03-30 (opened): https://www.hsa.gov.sg/other-regulations/controlled-drugs-psychotropic-substances/controlled-drugs/apply/

Handling must comply with MDA/regulations; import, storage, wholesale, export and manufacture require licences. Relevant storage/wholesale/manufacture licence holders must be registered pharmacists. A therapeutic-product import licence is product/consignment-specific, generally six months, alongside relevant dealer approval. Treat a Singapore university/hospital sponsor, IRB, regulatory consultation, protocol classification, lawful GMP supply and controlled-drug permissions as a proposed research sequence, not evidence of assured approval, timetable or costs.

Access limitation: Singapore Statutes Online returned HTTP 403 for https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL/MDA1973-RG1?ProvIds=Sc4-&ValidDate=20260601 . Search results exposed Schedule 4 text but it was not relied on as fully read law. Parent may open through the signed-in browser if exact statutory classification/exemptions are needed. Do not confuse MDA class/schedules with the differently numbered Regulations schedules.

## Strategic transactions: what the money actually proves

### R11 — Lilly / AtaiBeckley: verified, high confidence

Announcement dated 2026-07-16, opened/read: https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-acquire-ataibeckley-advance-therapies-treatment-resistant

The definitive agreement offers $6.75/share cash, about $2.8 billion aggregate equity value, plus a nontradeable CVR worth up to $2.50/share or approximately $1.0 billion aggregate. Contingent payments depend on clinical/regulatory milestones; they are not cash paid at closing. Lead program BPL-003 is investigational intranasal mebufotenin benzoate (5-MeO-DMT) for TRD. At announcement, closing was expected Q3 2026 subject to conditions.

Completion dated 2026-09-11, opened: https://lilly.gcs-web.com/node/54901

Requested SEC evidence, dated 2026-10-29, opened/read: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000059478/000005947826000104/q326lillysalesandearningsp.htm . It explicitly says Q3 business-development activity included the completed AtaiBeckley acquisition. Thus “completed in Q3” is supported, rather than merely an old forecast. This is evidence of strategic willingness to buy psychiatric drug-development assets, not evidence for a pair-bond drug market or successful BPL-003 approval. Do not treat $3.8 billion maximum as realised transaction cash.

### R12 — AbbVie / Gilgamesh: verified, high confidence

Announcement dated 2025-08-25, opened/read: https://news.abbvie.com/2025-08-25-AbbVie-to-Acquire-Gilgamesh-Pharmaceuticals-Bretisilocin,-a-Novel,-Investigational-Therapy-for-Major-Depressive-Disorder,-Expanding-Psychiatry-Pipeline

Agreement covers bretisilocin/GM-2505, then a Phase 2 investigational short-acting psychedelic for MDD, for up to $1.2 billion inclusive of upfront and development milestones. Employees and other programs (including GM-1020 and an existing AbbVie collaboration) move into an independent Gilgamesh Pharma entity. Therefore “AbbVie bought the entire continuing Gilgamesh pipeline for $1.2 billion upfront” is wrong.

SEC Q3 2025 10-Q, opened/read: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1551152/000155115225000049/abbv-20250930.htm , Note 4: completion after September 30; approximately $900 million upfront for equity and up to $300 million additional development milestones. Q4 accounting would follow. Issuer annual-report discovery identifies October 2025 completion, but exact day was not verified; use “October 2025” only after opening the annual report, or safely “completed after September 30 2025” from the read 10-Q. This validates pharmaceutical interest in MDD assets, not bonding efficacy or couples-market demand.

## Remaining verification gates before an operational legal-path claim

1. Check the chosen US oxytocin API identity, USP status, exact finished formulation, state rules, current shortage and any applicable 503B interim policy with the dispensing pharmacy and regulatory counsel. No specific compounder's compliance is established here.
2. Retrieve current FDA product database records if claiming exhaustive absence of nasal approval; preserve the historical Syntocinon caveat.
3. If discussing Lykos beyond the 2024 event, verify latest applicant name, trial/resubmission status and FDA record separately. This slice does not assert a November 2026 comprehensive status.
4. Obtain Singapore statutory text through an accessible official route and HSA protocol-specific guidance before describing a controlled-drug clinical study as feasible. No estimated approval timeline/cost is sourced here.
5. Separate original 2023 Australian rules from later guidance refinements and check the chosen state/territory before proposing an Australian clinic.
