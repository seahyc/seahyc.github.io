# Execution path: earn the relationship-care company

Dated 3 November 2026. Everything described as a plan, allocation, gate or target below is a proposal, not completed work, an industry benchmark, a vendor quote or a promise of efficacy. Source-backed precedents are linked where they constrain the plan.

## Company boundary and staged sequence

Chosen route (d): a clinician-referred, non-drug relationship-skills service for independently consenting adults. Initial job hypothesis: help small couples practices get appropriate clients through structured between-session practice and follow-up measurement. Sell a bounded eight-week program and a usable progress handoff. The practice remains responsible for therapy and clinical decisions; a disclaimer does not remove legal obligations if the actual service practices therapy. No relationship scoring algorithm makes medical decisions. No medication supply, attachment manipulation or divorce-prevention claim.

This job is already served: [OurRelationship explicitly offers waitlist, warm-up and homework uses](https://www.ourrelationship.com/therapists/). Therefore the first product is a comparative paid test of an unmet local workflow, not a claim that homework software is novel. If an incumbent fits, seek a licensed distribution/implementation agreement or stop; permission, economics and curriculum rights are not assumed. Do not copy IBCT or proprietary program content without rights.

## Path to buyers and demand

First economic buyer: owner/clinical director of a small US couples practice with a recurring between-session or referral problem. Actual payer could instead be the couple; the pilot contract must resolve whether the practice purchases seats, the couple pays, or a sponsor covers them. The model assumes net company revenue per paid enrolled couple after negotiated channel arrangements, with acquisition costs separately allocated; it assumes no referral kickback. Counsel reviews fee-splitting/inducement rules before any practice payment.

Reach practices through a clinical cofounder's network, IBCT training cohorts and professional referrals. [IBCT's training provider](https://ibct.org/courses/) and [OurRelationship's therapist page](https://www.ourrelationship.com/therapists/) establish a real practitioner ecosystem; they do not promise introductions, accreditation or access to a mailing list. Ask for a paid, bounded cohort plus access to deidentified operational baseline, a named clinical owner and renewal decision date. Ask why the incumbent cannot do it. No bulk scraping or unsolicited outreach has occurred.

Second buyer only after repeatability: EAP/mental-behavioral-health organization clinical/product director, then procurement/privacy/security. [OurRelationship markets directly to those buyers](https://www.ourrelationship.com/businesses/), a channel precedent and direct competitor. Sell a funded limited rollout with agreed eligible population, enrollment denominator, outcome measures, incident duties and renewal price. A demo or letter of intent is not reimbursed demand. Do not claim health-cost savings from relationship-score changes. Employer receives only properly protected aggregate reporting; employment eligibility must not depend on a partner consenting.

Optional drug-infrastructure buyer: psychiatrist/site director with actual authorised patients in Australia, or an approved research sponsor. MAPS's [2026 statement](https://maps.org/2026/08/10/maps-responds-to-report-of-progress-for-mdma-assisted-therapy-for-ptsd-with-fda/) identifies therapist preparation, integration and education as existing activities. This is not an empty training market. Ask for a paid workflow problem and product-specific responsibility map before any platform build.

## Path to credibility

Recruit a clinical cofounder with couples-treatment and intimate-partner-violence screening experience, then an independent statistician and safeguarding advisor. Publish a carefully bounded evidence review, pilot protocol, participant flow, missing outcomes and failures. Do not put mechanistic hormone claims in marketing. Benchmark against a purchasable alternative and measure workflow cost as well as satisfaction. Establish curriculum rights, privacy agreements, liability cover and a named accountable clinician before onboarding. These are proposed trust-building steps, not endorsements already obtained.

The science to learn: dyadic measurement (two people are not independent samples), trial allocation, attrition, clinically meaningful change, deterioration, distinctions between communication, relationship satisfaction, PTSD symptoms and separation. A biological biomarker is not a surrogate for relationship health. [The 2020 digital-program RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32134290/) found improved functioning without significant breakup reduction, demonstrating why the endpoint choice matters.

Team sourcing: clinical-research authors and university couples-treatment labs are expertise pools, not assumed recruits. IBCT course networks supply a practical education route but training alone does not confer licensure. Engineering needs privacy/access control competence and health workflow experience; no AI therapist is necessary for the first test. A later Rx branch needs an experienced CNS drug-development/translational pharmacology cofounder, CMC/GMP, clinical operations, regulatory counsel and protected IP; this is a different staffing and capital plan.

## Capital path and constraints

Order: capped founder funds/appropriately informed angels → collected pilot/service revenue → health-services angels/seed after renewals → sponsor/strategic distribution or a properly scoped research grant → venture only after repeatable economics and measurable differentiation. No debt against unproved future therapy demand. The amounts above are allocation scenarios with no supplier quotes. Grants are optional and timing/eligibility must be checked.

[NIH's March 2026 SBIR/STTR policy](https://grants.nih.gov/grants/policy/nihgps/HTML5/section_18/18.5.2_eligibility.htm) requires an eligible US small business, ownership/control qualifications and largely US performance; an ordinary Singapore-owned startup is not automatically eligible. Do not manufacture nominal US ownership. The current solicitation, research eligibility and collaboration structure need separate verification; no award is assumed in the runway.

Pharma exit comparisons: [Lilly's acquisition](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000059478/000005947826000104/q326lillysalesandearningsp.htm) buys psychiatric development assets, and [AbbVie describes a Phase 2 MDD asset](https://news.abbvie.com/2025-08-25-AbbVie-to-Acquire-Gilgamesh-Pharmaceuticals-Bretisilocin,-a-Novel,-Investigational-Therapy-for-Major-Depressive-Disorder,-Expanding-Psychiatry-Pipeline). Neither is a comparable multiple for a relationship-support service. There is no verified acquisition multiple or sale probability for the chosen wedge in this dossier.

## Actual regulatory/legal/ethical route

US initial service: counsel classifies intended use under [FDA's January 2026 wellness policy](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/general-wellness-policy-low-risk-devices); policy does not apply to drugs or exempt clinical practice, consumer protection or privacy. If actual functions diagnose/treat illness, reconsider device/professional rules. Licensed clinical delivery, where present, needs the relevant state/patient-location analysis; this research grants no cross-state practice rights. Assess HIPAA covered/business-associate status and relevant state privacy laws; outside HIPAA is not outside privacy regulation, as [FTC's health-app rule explains](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-breach-notification-rule-basics-business).

US drug branch: approved finished products, compounded formulations, IND research and future marketing approvals are different legal objects. Review a specific formulation, pharmacy, API and state rules under 503A/503B; do not use a prescription as evidence of efficacy. MDMA remains a DEA Schedule I research/access question in the sources checked. [The 2026 executive order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/04/accelerating-medical-treatments-for-serious-mental-illness/) accelerates specified processes; it does not make a commercial couples clinic lawful. [FDA's actual 2024 CRL](https://download.open.fda.gov/crl/CRL_NDA215455_20240808.pdf) demands better safety characterization, durability and bias control. No drug-trial launch is proposed or budgeted here.

Australia: [the TGA route](https://www.tga.gov.au/resources/guidance/accessing-mdma-34-methylenedioxy-methamphetamine-and-psilocybine-psychiatrist) begins with specialist psychiatrist, HREC-approved protocol and product-specific AP approval, plus state/territory permissions and lawful supply; MDMA for PTSD and psilocybine for TRD. Unapproved-goods access does not authorise relationship enhancement, partner dosing absent eligibility, ordinary retail supply or a telehealth bonding subscription. Non-drug software requires its own intended-use/privacy/practice classification before expansion.

Singapore: start with [HSA intended-use classification](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/medical-devices/digital-health/), PDPA/professional-scope review and local clinical partner before any service; importing US permissions is invalid. Controlled-drug human research would need [HSA trial/CRM route](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/other-regulations/clinical-trials/overview/), ethics and controlled-drug permissions, not a consumer product launch. Exact statutory exemptions and product-specific authorisation remain unverified. Singapore is not the proposed medication-clinic beachhead.

Ethical product rule: optimize freely chosen well-being and safe agency. Independent sober consent, private screening for coercion/abuse, independent withdrawal, no covert administration, no employer/partner surveillance, no pressured romantic or sexual decisions, accessible crisis referral and independent adverse-outcome monitoring. Safety screening is not merely a conversion hurdle. A safe decision to separate can be a successful outcome. These are design requirements inferred from the risks and research exclusions, not proven safeguards eliminating risk.

## Founder payout and realistic personal outcome

First quarter: assumed $9k founder draw is compensation funded from the cash cap, not profit. At small scale the founder may earn a salary only if revenue/funding supports it; no dividend while obligations and safety reserves are unfunded. In the unit model, 3,000 paid enrollments/year produce $387,150 contribution before $300k assumed fixed cost, leaving $87,150 before tax, extraordinary loss and financing. This is a small-business outcome; it cannot support a venture valuation by itself.

At larger scale, dividends require actual distributable profits and reserves; venture-funded growth usually reinvests and dilutes equity. A secondary requires investors/buyers willing and permitted to buy; an exit requires a strategic reason, transferable rights and consent-compliant data use. No forced cross-sale of couple data creates a legitimate data moat. Illustrative ownership arithmetic only: 30% ownership of a $50m equity exit yields $15m gross before preferences, tax and costs; no probability or valuation is asserted, and preferences can reduce common proceeds sharply. This scenario is not based on Lilly/AbbVie multiples.

## Stop/go gates that can overturn the plan

- Day 30: no residual paid problem after incumbent comparison → stop building; seek distribution partnership only if independently attractive.
- Day 60: no three paid scopes at viable economics → stop or reprice once, without confusing free pilots with demand.
- Day 90: fewer than two practice renewals or support above base 1h/couple without enough price → pause expansion; finish follow-up at day 120–150.
- Month 12: no repeated channel, positive contribution after fully loaded acquisition, or credible independent evaluation design → do not raise venture money on a drug narrative.
- Evaluation: preregister meaningful outcome threshold with clinicians/statistician; missing data, deterioration and safety must be reported. No arbitrary pilot score threshold establishes clinical efficacy. If no incremental clinical benefit, sell only a proven delivery advantage if buyers pay for it.
- Any stage: serious safety concern or consent/privacy failure → pause affected enrollment/delivery for independent review; retain access to crisis care; stop if unresolved. Zero observed incidents is not proof of safety.
- Drug option: no indication, no differentiated human signal, no rights or no lawful protocol → do not open the option. Staying a non-drug business is a valid endpoint.

## Gate 1 corrections applied · 3 November 2026

1. **No TAM/distress denominator.** This dossier establishes neither a couples-distress population nor a lifetime-divorce denominator. Divorce events are context only, never an addressable market or demand estimate.
2. **FDA letter scope.** Public prose will use the confirmed reliability-of-safety-data finding from the 8 August 2024 CRL (https://download.open.fda.gov/crl/CRL_NDA215455_20240808.pdf). Broader durability/expectancy descriptions in research notes are not public headline claims without section-specific attribution.
3. **Learning service, not self-financing.** Month-12 target of 100 paid enrolments creates only $12,905 contribution before fixed costs in the assumed base model; annual break-even is 2,325 enrolments. The pilot cannot self-finance this company or a drug programme.
4. **Price and deal provenance.** OurRelationship $249/$99/$79 menus were collected by the research agent, not re-verified by the parent cross-model review. They are dated observations, not checkout guarantees. Lilly's $4.2bn combined IPR&D item is not the AtaiBeckley deal price; the filed deal is approximately $2.8bn upfront plus up to $1bn contingent.
