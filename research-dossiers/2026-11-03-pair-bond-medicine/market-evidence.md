## Decision and four routes

The possibility is helping willing couples create a better relationship, including when one partner has a genuine illness. The investable unit is not “all divorces prevented.” Drug efficacy, legally available care, a workflow sold to a clinic and a consumer habit program are different companies.

| Route | Who buys / value acquired | Evidence and execution constraints | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) Rx oxytocin-system/entactogen asset for relationship-adjacent illness | Pharma licenses/acquires differentiated clinical assets; patient/health system ultimately buys indicated benefit | PTSD with partner is the most directly evidenced adjacent wedge here; postpartum bonding and social anxiety require their own reviews. Need target engagement, formulation, safety, indication-specific efficacy, IP/FTO and manufacturing. Uncontrolled couple pilots cannot establish added drug benefit. Depression acquisitions are real but do not price this asset | Do not start now without a scientific cofounder, differentiated rights and a financeable decisive experiment. No candidate selected |
| (b) Medication + couples-therapy clinic/telehealth | Patient/self-pay sponsor purchases supervised care | US compounding is not product approval; licensed practice and pharmacy/state obligations remain. An approved drug for a partner's real illness is not an approved bonding treatment. Australian MDMA access is narrowly PTSD-specific; Singapore is controlled. Labour, facilities, screening and liability bind margins | Reject oxytocin prescription funnel. Conventional care for a real indication can be lawful, but relationship-enhancement marketing is unsupported |
| (c) Infrastructure for entactogen-assisted care | Authorised psychiatrist/site, trial sponsor, later payer/clinic network | Existing protocols, training providers, EHRs and site workflows; US access timetable uncertain. Australia creates a present, restricted buyer population, not broad retail demand. No verified site-count × budget model | Watch/validate separately; do not fund a platform on assumed FDA approval. No demonstrated missing infrastructure job yet |
| (d) Non-drug relationship support with measured outcomes | Couples, referring practice, later EAP/benefits intermediary | Randomized digital-program evidence, but incumbent prices and distribution already exist. Must prove residual job and paid differentiation; cannot inherit incumbent efficacy | Selected for capped discovery/pilot only. Build service → repeat workflow → evaluate → sponsor distribution; venture scale conditional |

Best alternative to founding any of these may be distributing an existing evidence-based program. A decision to stop is valid. The selected wedge is deliberately vulnerable to an incumbent comparison; no evidence presently establishes that it beats OurRelationship.

| Oxytocin is totally unregulated | Contradicted by prescription Pitocin label and compounding framework. “Not a controlled substance” and “not regulated” are different claims |
| Mammalian bonding is well understood | Biology is real; receptor-null vole results and human trial limitations defeat a simple master-switch/product inference |
| Can buy a kilogram at Sigma | A real catalog product exists; the checked O3251 menu lists IU packages and a custom-order option, not a verified kilogram offer. Research reagent availability is not legal/safe administration |
| Used for women not bonding with children/husbands | No approved relationship/bonding indication established by checked labels. Obstetric bleeding/labour indications and historical milk let-down are different from emotional attachment. Research/off-label use is not established efficacy |
| Used for autistic children who struggle with bonding | Clinical research/use exists; the large 2021 RCT found no significant primary social-function advantage. Autism social impairment is not adult romantic pair bonding |
| Does not form chemical dependency / unburdens dependency | No long-term dependence/withdrawal dataset for the proposed repeated-use couple product verified. Endogenous does not mean harmless. Do not affirm or convert this into a safety claim; MDMA has separate abuse-potential concerns |
| People should stay together for children | Normative claim, not a product endpoint. Staying in an unsafe or coerced relationship must not count as success |

## Science source notes and translation boundaries

1. **Ditzen et al. (2009)**, [original author-hosted paper](https://www.psychologie.uzh.ch/dam/jcr:00000000-1563-1d5c-0000-00004d338ca0/BiolPsychiatry-CoupleConflict09.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsych.2008.10.011): double-blind randomized experiment in 47 heterosexual couples. Acute coded conflict behaviour and cortisol changed. A laboratory discussion is several inferential steps from durable voluntary relationship health; no divorce-prevention endpoint.
2. **Walum, Waldman & Young (2016)**, [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4690817/): statistical analysis of then-existing intranasal literature found low power and bias risk. Its modeled power percentages are not false-positive rates and not a current census. Main reviewer also retrieved the power sections directly. Prefer the qualitative replication caution in public narrative unless exact estimates are necessary.
3. **Leng & Ludwig (2016)**, [accepted manuscript](https://www.pure.ed.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/19764306/accepted_manuscript_Leng_and_Ludwig.pdf): delivery, peripheral effects, target engagement and assays complicate interpretation. This is a serious technical critique, not proof that every future formulation fails.
4. **Sikich et al. (2021)**, [author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34644471/): 290 randomized children/adolescents, 24 weeks; modified-intention-to-treat 277. Primary difference −0.2 points, 95% CI −1.5 to 1.0, P=.61. Strong translation warning; no direct adult-couple null inference. NEJM full text blocked, abstract read and independently checked by lead.
5. **Berendzen et al. (2023)**, [full paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10150797/): receptor-null prairie voles retained partner preferences and parenting. Constitutive knockout permits developmental compensation. Neither “one love switch” nor “oxytocin irrelevant” follows.
6. **Monson/Wagner et al. (2020)**, [full paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7748057/), [author copy](https://www.coupletherapyforptsd.com/wp-content/uploads/Monson-et-al-2020.pdf): six couples, one PTSD patient per couple, both partners received MDMA in an intensive conjoint protocol. Within-group PTSD and relationship improvements; no control. Separating medicine from therapy, expectancy, selection and attention is impossible here. No serious adverse events in this tiny sample cannot establish rare-event safety.
7. **Morland et al. (2026)**, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/jts.70126), [Europe PMC author-abstract record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=EXT_ID:42693902&format=json&resultType=core): eight veteran-partner dyads, single-site open-label, no serious adverse events reported; improvements with some rebound at follow-up. Abstract-only access; no full-text methods audit. This prevents a stale “only six couples have been studied” claim. Lead re-fetch returned 503; collection agent successfully read abstract; preserve this boundary and avoid precise effects unless needed.
8. **NCT06044675**, [official trial](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06044675), [API](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT06044675): recruiting randomized open-label phase 2, planned 60 individuals, crossover after CBCT-only follow-up, no posted results. Last posted update 19 August 2026; estimated completion 31 March 2027. Lead independently re-fetched official JSON. Neither 60 completed subjects nor 60 couples nor a blinded efficacy result.
9. **Monson et al. (2012)**, [original CBCT RCT](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4404628/): 40 couples, CBCT versus waitlist; patient PTSD and patient relationship satisfaction improved. No attention-matched comparator, small sample and severe-aggression exclusion. Demonstrates a non-drug therapeutic component requiring a fair comparator in drug trials, not a cross-trial drug effect estimate.
10. **Doss et al. (2020)**, [author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32134290/): 742 low-income couples randomized to two web programs or waitlist. Functioning improved; breakup frequency did not significantly fall at six months. Abstract-level method/outcome verification only; publisher full text not required for these limited statements. This is the most relevant “what the headline omits” distinction for a non-drug company.

10a. **Broader PTSD evidence is stronger than the couple pilots:** [Mitchell et al. 2021 MAPP1](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-021-01336-3) randomized 90 participants; [2023 MAPP2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-023-02565-4) randomized 104 to MDMA or placebo with the same therapy. MAPP2 reported 18-week CAPS-5 change −23.7 versus −14.8 (reported P<.001). These are published controlled PTSD symptom findings, not romantic-bond/divorce outcomes or conjoint-treatment trials. The actual FDA CRL challenges safety characterization, durability and bias in this development package. Do not adopt the papers' broad safe/well-tolerated conclusion as a regulator-accepted fact. Conversely, do not describe all MDMA psychiatric evidence as uncontrolled. Lead opened both primary articles and read abstracts; 2023 page links an October 2024 correction. Subsequent body/PDF retrieval failed; keep public numerical claims to the read current abstract.

## Market size without market theatre

No dependable public “couples therapy market size” was verified. Syndicated market reports often combine categories and keep methods behind payment; no figures are inferred. Bottom-up attainable sales require named practices × eligible referrals × both-partner consent × paid activation × net company price. None of those conversion/volume inputs has been observed here. Report stock/event statistics only with denominators.

The model shows 30,000 annual enrollments at assumed $299 gives $8.97m gross revenue; that is arithmetic, not evidence that this volume is reachable. A hypothetical $100m annual gross-revenue company at that unchanged price needs 334,449 enrollments/year; budgets and fixed costs would not stay constant. Venture-scale economics require a distribution engine, measurable differentiation and repeatable contribution; the evidence does not yet establish them. Drug companies instead monetize rights to an indication-specific asset and face very different capital-at-risk, timeline and failure probabilities.

## Gate 1 corrections applied · 3 November 2026

1. **No TAM/distress denominator.** This dossier establishes neither a couples-distress population nor a lifetime-divorce denominator. Divorce events are context only, never an addressable market or demand estimate.
2. **FDA letter scope.** Public prose will use the confirmed reliability-of-safety-data finding from the 8 August 2024 CRL (https://download.open.fda.gov/crl/CRL_NDA215455_20240808.pdf). Broader durability/expectancy descriptions in research notes are not public headline claims without section-specific attribution.
3. **Learning service, not self-financing.** Month-12 target of 100 paid enrolments creates only $12,905 contribution before fixed costs in the assumed base model; annual break-even is 2,325 enrolments. The pilot cannot self-finance this company or a drug programme.
4. **Price and deal provenance.** OurRelationship $249/$99/$79 menus were collected by the research agent, not re-verified by the parent cross-model review. They are dated observations, not checkout guarantees. Lilly's $4.2bn combined IPR&D item is not the AtaiBeckley deal price; the filed deal is approximately $2.8bn upfront plus up to $1bn contingent.
