# Evidence ledger: pair-bond medicine

Access date for every entry: 3 November 2026. Primary evidence unless otherwise marked. Confidence concerns the scoped claim, not success of a company. Facts are reported observations/rules; estimates are model-dependent estimates in a source; assumptions are invented planning inputs; inferences connect evidence; scenarios are arithmetic. No hypothesis is promoted to demonstrated efficacy or paid demand. Detailed read/access notes: market-evidence.md, science-notes.md, regulatory-notes.md. The main ledger and market-evidence.md supersede collection-note limitations specifically corrected below.

## E02 · Acute oxytocin couple signal
- Type: fact, original randomized experiment, Ditzen et al. 2009. Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsych.2008.10.011 ; full author copy https://www.psychologie.uzh.ch/dam/jcr:00000000-1563-1d5c-0000-00004d338ca0/BiolPsychiatry-CoupleConflict09.pdf
- Confidence: high for described experiment. 47 heterosexual couples; acute laboratory conflict behaviour/cortisol findings.
- Proves: an acute effect in that experiment. Does not prove: durable affection, clinical benefit or divorce prevention.
- Counterevidence: small healthy sample and proxy endpoints; replication/delivery critiques E03. Impact: mechanistic promise, not a target-product profile.

## E03 · Reliability and intranasal delivery are material uncertainties
- Type: estimates/analysis in Walum et al. 2016; scientific critique in Leng & Ludwig 2016. Sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4690817/ ; https://www.pure.ed.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/19764306/accepted_manuscript_Leng_and_Ludwig.pdf
- Confidence: high for authors' arguments, medium for extrapolation to a new formulation. Walum modeled healthy-study power 16%, clinical power 12% for then-included effect/sample assumptions.
- Proves: credible reasons to distrust vote-counted small positive studies and to require target engagement/replication. Does not prove: those percentages are false-positive rates or all newer trials/formulations fail.
- Counterevidence: genuine oxytocin effects remain possible; improved delivery can be researched. Impact: replication, dose/target-engagement and meaningful human endpoint gates before asset funding.

## E04 · Large autism study was null on primary social endpoint
- Type: fact, author abstract of Sikich et al., published 14 October 2021. Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34644471/ ; DOI https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2103583
- Confidence: high for abstract-level result. 290 enrolled/randomized, 277 modified ITT; 24 weeks; difference −0.2 points, 95% CI −1.5 to 1.0, P=.61.
- Proves: no significant between-group primary benefit in this population/regimen. Does not prove: direct adult-couple null or universal oxytocin inefficacy.
- Counterevidence: different population and endpoint. Impact: clinical-use anecdotes and small trials cannot carry the marriage thesis. Full NEJM methods not independently audited.

## E05 · Receptor-null voles still bond
- Type: fact, Berendzen et al., online 27 January 2023. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10150797/ ; https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2022.12.011
- Confidence: high for study result. Three independent null lines retain partner preference/substantial parenting; assay sample sizes detailed in science notes.
- Proves: receptor indispensability story is too simple. Does not prove: acute pharmacology ineffective, no role for oxytocin or direct human transfer.
- Counterevidence: developmental compensation/redundancy possible. Impact: no master-switch metaphor presented as fact.

## E06 · First MDMA-conjoint pilot is encouraging and uncontrolled
- Type: fact, Monson/Wagner et al. 7 December 2020. Sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7748057/ ; https://www.coupletherapyforptsd.com/wp-content/uploads/Monson-et-al-2020.pdf
- Confidence: high for reported pilot, low for causal incremental medicine effect. Six couples; one PTSD patient/couple, both partners treated; within-group symptoms/relationship improvements, no serious adverse events reported.
- Proves: feasibility/signal in selected intensive protocol. Does not prove: added value beyond therapy/expectancy, broad safety or benefit to healthy couples.
- Counterevidence: no control, selected homogeneous sample, tiny harm denominator. Impact: choose genuine PTSD context for future drug hypothesis; no efficacy marketing.

## E09 · Non-drug conjoint therapy is an active scientific alternative

## Gate 1 corrections applied · 3 November 2026

1. **No TAM/distress denominator.** This dossier establishes neither a couples-distress population nor a lifetime-divorce denominator. Divorce events are context only, never an addressable market or demand estimate.
2. **FDA letter scope.** Public prose will use the confirmed reliability-of-safety-data finding from the 8 August 2024 CRL (https://download.open.fda.gov/crl/CRL_NDA215455_20240808.pdf). Broader durability/expectancy descriptions in research notes are not public headline claims without section-specific attribution.
3. **Learning service, not self-financing.** Month-12 target of 100 paid enrolments creates only $12,905 contribution before fixed costs in the assumed base model; annual break-even is 2,325 enrolments. The pilot cannot self-finance this company or a drug programme.
4. **Price and deal provenance.** OurRelationship $249/$99/$79 menus were collected by the research agent, not re-verified by the parent cross-model review. They are dated observations, not checkout guarantees. Lilly's $4.2bn combined IPR&D item is not the AtaiBeckley deal price; the filed deal is approximately $2.8bn upfront plus up to $1bn contingent.
