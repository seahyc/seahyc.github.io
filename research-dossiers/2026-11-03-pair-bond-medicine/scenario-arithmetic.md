## Equations and interpretation

delivery contribution = price - support hours * loaded wage - platform/payment - price * refund fraction
after-acquisition contribution = delivery contribution - fully loaded acquisition allocation
annual break-even = ceil(annual fixed company cost / positive after-acquisition contribution)
operating result = paid enrolled couples * after-acquisition contribution - annual fixed company cost

## Boundaries

No TAM or distress denominator established. Month-12 100-enrollment gate is learning, not self-financing: base contribution 12905 before fixed costs versus 2325 annual break-even enrollments.
Every paid enrollment incurs acquisition and expected delivery cost, including dropouts; refunds reserved separately.
Support allowance includes screening, onboarding, reminders, outcome collection and routine escalation; severe cases can exceed it.
Fixed cost is incremental central product, clinical governance, legal/security, sales management and administration; variable staff acquisition and support are outside it. Do not double-count the same hours.
No recurring subscription or retention-derived LTV is assumed; referral conversion and sponsorship remain unvalidated.
Excluded: tax, financing, founder equity value, extraordinary liability, major trial budget, pharmaceutical R&D and clinic capex.
First-90-day budget is a separate cash spending envelope, not added to annual fixed cost mechanically. Replace all allocations with scoped quotes; pilot income is not precommitted funding.
At 30000 couples fixed costs will change; constant-fixed scenario is arithmetic only, not a scale forecast.
