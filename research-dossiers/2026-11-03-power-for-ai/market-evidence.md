No paid research subscription, private customer evidence, supplier quotation, engineering design review or customer contact. Supplemental collection is in nuclear-sources.md and onsite-sources.md; **the calibrated master claims are evidence-ledger.md**. A historical report or undated product page is not a live firm quote.

## Decision synthesis

The exciting possibility is plentiful dependable electricity wherever useful computing belongs. The practical question is which constraint a new company can actually remove. A power plant makes energy, a network delivers it, equipment changes voltage and isolates faults, and operators grant service subject to system conditions. Each has its own lead time and authority. The proposed first company sells an engineer-reviewed readiness package at the junction of equipment, service milestones and developer decisions. That recommendation is an inference about scope and testability; the public sources do not show that customers will buy it.

Nuclear progress deserves a serious account. DOE reports the criticality milestone achieved, with Valar among named test reactors, but its original outside-national-laboratories condition and later INL-inclusive tally are not reconciled. Ignoring the reported test achievements would be stale; whether they satisfy every original geographic condition remains unresolved. Calling those criticality tests commercial power would also be wrong. Reactor peer evaluation needs separate columns for project identity, authorization type, actual fueled operation, sustained net electrical output, licensed commercial site and delivered contract—not one startup “progress score”.

## Nuclear and onsite comparison — notes to preserve in later narrative

Detailed source URLs, dates and limitations are in nuclear-sources.md / onsite-sources.md and E12–E28. Main mechanisms are not interchangeable:

- Turbine/engine: convert fuel heat to shaft work and electricity; must deliver net dependable output under actual ambient conditions and changing load. OEM backlog is cumulative equipment/reservation stock, not annual factory output.
- Fuel cell: electrochemical conversion; a natural-gas unit remains fuel-dependent and is not automatically carbon-free. Equinix/Bloom is named operational precedent, still vendor-reported and supplemental to grid.
- Battery: stored energy supplies a bounded period, useful for load swings/ride-through and flexible service. It does not make the charge energy. Installed MW, MWh, state of charge, reserve and degradation must be distinguished.
- Reactor: chain reaction makes heat; removal of heat and conversion to useful net electricity, sustained operation, fuel supply, site approval, waste and operating organisation remain separate jobs. MWt is thermal power and MWe is electrical output. Criticality can be demonstrated at very low thermal power.
- Restart/uprate: existing site/plant pathway, distinct from new technology; does not eliminate readiness and licensing requirements.
- Enhanced geothermal: underground heat and engineered permeability, with well and project execution risk. Fervo's 2028 PPA is future supply; EGS should not be labelled a demonstrated superhot-rock system.

Existing geothermal Fieldbook canonical title-derived route verified from front matter: [Clean, Limitless Energy: Superhot Geothermal](/research/clean-limitless-energy-superhot-geothermal/). Its existing alias `/research/superhot-rock-geothermal/` also resolves by convention. Link it in Phase 2 rather than duplicating it or using its old facts as fresh evidence.

## Public/paywalled boundary and valuable missing evidence

Public: laboratory queue summary and downloadable CC BY dataset; DOE/NREL summary; issuer financial/technical releases; customer announcements; product pages; IMDA official factsheet through Dia. These support mechanism, offered alternatives, regulatory/physical milestone categories and supply response.

Not accessed: WoodMac full surveys/pricing distributions, SemiAnalysis subscriber reports/models, BloombergNEF full forecasts, private utility offers, OEM allocation books, EPC bids, PPAs' detailed prices/conditions, site electrical designs, delivery guarantees, delay damages, debt term sheets and project cash flows. Search snippets and press descriptions are not access. No conclusions are inferred from inaccessible paid material. No paid provider discovery pass requested or conducted.

The most valuable paid diligence would obtain comparable **firm** quote cohorts with specification, currency, geography, order date, deposit, guaranteed delivery, testing, warranty and installation boundary; real large-load applications with deposits/site control and deduplication; actual net power/uptime and fuel/service invoices; and PPA credit, curtailment, delay and termination terms. A public reactor pipeline total cannot answer these.

## Primary research plan

Obtain lawful access to the buyer's last failed or delayed site, vendor quotations and utility correspondence; bring independent engineer to compare best available existing workflow. Trace the first actual error and whether correcting it changes the true critical path. Measure collected fee, full delivery/CAC hours, liability scope and repeat use. Build no proprietary study solver, warehouse or plant until the distinct business case warrants it. The market model in scenario-model.json is a falsification tool, not evidence that assumed fees will clear procurement.
