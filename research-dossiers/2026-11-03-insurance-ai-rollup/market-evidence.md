## 1. Market depth: three denominators that must not be conflated
**Singapore insurer premiums:** GIA's 25 March 2026 release reports 2025 domestic general GWP S$6.09bn and domestic+offshore S$11.2bn. Source body opened: https://gia.org.sg/agents/media-center/company-news/592-gi-results-2025.html (lines 58–74). This establishes insurer-market activity. It includes personal lines and business not available to a startup, and offshore business is different in scope. Do not label either sum commercial broker revenue or acquisition TAM. GIA is an industry association, not a commission register.

**Hong Kong licence stock:** IA's dated 30 September 2026 series reports 814 broker companies. General-only 85; general+long-term excluding linked 160; general+long-term including linked 433. Derived general permission count 678. https://www.ia.org.hk/en/infocenter/statistics/statistics.html (lines 43–50 opened). These are permissions, not production, independence or sellers. Do not use 814 as commercial broker count or apply a notional EBITDA to each. HK general-business aggregate premium PDF located but body access failed; headline premium number withheld pending primary read (needs-browser.md).

**Taiwan broker-channel economics:** TII index https://www.tii.org.tw/tii/english/rd/importantIndices/ links Table20, index dated 22 July 2026. Direct source https://www.tii.org.tw/tii/export/sites/tii/information/files/20_202605.pdf read by public download and pdftotext, with headers and footnotes. For 2025: 431 total organisations/persons, 48 non-life only, 79 life only, 304 both; non-life broker-channel written premium NT$66.105954bn and commission income NT$7.432572bn; stated non-life market share 23.05%. Derived commission/premium ratio ~11.24%. Includes persons and companies, all ownership types and non-life lines; no independent commercial seller pool inferred. The ratio is aggregate reported channel commission/premium, not a quoted deal's take rate or comprehensive net revenue. Avoid cross-country currency aggregation without a dated FX convention.

### The acquisition funnel to measure
Regulator list → currently operating commercial general brokerage → deduplicated ultimate ownership → independent seller → transferable people/carrier/client relationships → normalized cash earnings → acceptable price → approvable and financeable acquisition. No public source in this pass supplies the last five filters across all three countries. Multinational service competition is evidenced by Aon's Singapore offering and Howden's local terms; a quantified multinational market-share claim is unverified and omitted.

## 2. Commission mechanics, client money and claims reality
Howden Singapore's current terms §§2,6,7 describe policyholder claims negotiations, potential insurer-side limited services/conflict controls, premiums accounts and commission/fee/profit-share remuneration. https://www.howdengroup.com/sg-en/howden-terms-business (read lines 437–447,509–529). They establish an actual local bundled service model; no numeric universal commission or recovery fee follows. AIG Singapore separates broker/agent commissions from its agent contingent-compensation wording: https://www.aig.sg/content/dam/aig/apac/singapore/documents/other/producer_compensation_disclosures.pdf . Do not expand the agent clause to a blanket broker entitlement.

HKIA remuneration practice note requires client disclosure; its footnote treats volume/profit commissions and other payments separately and says additional disclosure/express consent may be required. https://www.ia.org.hk/english/legislative_framework/circulars/reg_matters/files/Practice_note_on_Remuneration_Disclosure.pdf . This is evidence of conflict/disclosure obligations, not a market rate. Earnings diligence must separate ordinary commissions, fees, contingent commissions, one-offs and cancellation/clawback exposure. Exclude speculative contingent commissions from debt service.

Claims advocacy helps a policyholder establish coverage, quantify damage, notify correctly and negotiate with its insurer. Subrogation pursues another liable party under an insurer's recovery right after indemnity. A broker may help identify facts without owning that right. Taiwan Insurance Act Article53 is direct legal evidence; SG/HK examples in regulatory-notes.md show actual policy/case treatment with limited scope. Keep insurer and policyholder engagements distinct, avoid double charging for bundled assistance, and refer legal disputes to appropriately authorised counsel. No evidence establishes a transferable US-style health-recovery market in these Asian jurisdictions.

Vendor source establishes an offered workflow, not independent accuracy, price or local-language suitability. Appetite matching and renewals are proposed internal workflow extensions to test; no autonomous placement, binding or advice permission inferred.

Its Singapore claims-management page is geographically relevant: https://www.aon.com/en-sg/capabilities/claims-management . These show incumbents already combine service and tools, not a measured new-platform retention advantage.

Shift's product page offers structured/unstructured recovery detection, prioritization and alerts: https://www.shift-technology.com/claims-subrogation-software . Its superhuman-accuracy marketing has no shared dataset/denominator independently validated here and is excluded. Detection precision, incremental collectible cash and full cohort cost are the right test. Require source-linked extraction, human review, authority gating, audit history, local-language evaluations and lawful data processing.

We do not underwrite eight-times entry arbitrage from these pages. Model eight-times entry is a hypothetical seller offer to test, not established availability; 12x and14x equity sensitivities belong beside it.

Bank debt/private credit may fund verified acquisitions, but no startup financing quote obtained. Published incumbent borrowing and preferred rounds cannot establish a new founder's terms. Bridge facilities and larger total funding can be necessary before closing; never use client premiums as cash equity.

## 6. How wide must the company become?
At an assumed USD1bn EV, the model's 10–18x exit range requires USD55.6–100m EBITDA. At18–30% margins that spans USD185.2–555.6m net revenue (cross-combinations in JSON). A 14x/25% illustration needs USD71.43m EBITDA and USD285.71m net revenue. These are inverse valuation equations, not market estimates. They establish the earnings burden; they cannot prove that Singapore/Hong Kong/Taiwan can or cannot supply it.

The defensible answer is conditional: SG is an initial learning/operating market; HK/Taiwan deepen the corridor only if locally measured books fit. Wider APAC is the likely scale search if obtainable earnings fall short—an inference, not an inevitability established by TAM. Wholesale/reinsurance and MGA/program businesses can access different value pools but also add carrier/capacity and underwriting responsibilities. No verified public universal 20–25% MGA take or higher valuation premium is assumed. Test each adjacency separately rather than buying all three categories to reach a headline.

## 7. What the headline omits
- GWP passes to insurers; it is not net revenue. Client cash is not acquisition finance. Reported contingent income can be volatile.
- Underwriters/clients may refuse transfer or leave; earnouts can preserve price discipline but create incentives to write poor-quality business.
- AI savings must survive integration, qualified review, cyber/PI and cash realization. No independent evidence here proves AI adds a fixed number of margin points.
- Borrowing magnifies both the book's durability and its loss. Earnout maximums, preference capital and tax reduce founder cash.
- Strategic/sponsor precedents establish historical appetite, not a buyer at an intended valuation/date. A softer rate cycle and lower exit multiple can offset operational growth.
