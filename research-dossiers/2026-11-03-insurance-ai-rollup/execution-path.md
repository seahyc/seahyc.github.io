## Buyers, access and procurement
**Broker sellers:** owner-managers of independently controlled general-commercial firms with a succession problem, durable insurer relationships and service staff. Use MAS/HKIA registers and corporate ownership records as lead lists, then approach through insurance accountants, professional-indemnity advisers, insurance recruiters and industry associations. These are proposed channels, not existing warm introductions. Ask for permissioned anonymized cohort records followed by a confidential data room; do not request client confidential files before a legal basis and secure handling are agreed.

**Policyholder buyer:** Singapore regional logistics/manufacturing company CFO/risk manager. Ask them to pay for a specified coverage/renewal/claims-preparation result where legally permitted. Test whether they prefer an existing broker's bundled service. Aon's Singapore claims offering is direct competition, not validation of an empty niche: https://www.aon.com/en-sg/capabilities/claims-management .

**Recovery buyer:** insurer claims/recovery head, a TPA acting with delegated authority, or a self-insured entity owning an enforceable claim. Start with commercial property/cargo files with documented third-party responsibility, not health-plan rules transplanted from the US. Request a tightly bounded closed-file review, signed mandate, baseline exclusion list, fee-on-incremental-cash terms and legal escalation protocol. No direct collection demands before qualified authorization.

**Channels:** the Singapore Reinsurers' Association's SIRC event is a real industry meeting route (https://www.sg-reinsurers.org.sg/features-event); current 2026 registration dates are already past as of this dossier, so do not put a future 2026 ticket purchase in a 90-day plan. Arrange follow-up industry meetings and future announced events, alongside the Singapore Insurance Brokers' Association and local professional networks after checking current access requirements. No attendance or member access assumed. Procurement requires claims/security/legal review, then a permissioned sample; 'closed files' still contain confidential/personal information.

## Credibility and team
The non-negotiable early partner is an experienced Singapore commercial broker/operator with insurer relationships and the ability to meet fit-and-proper expectations. Add a claims advocate with coverage/quantum experience; fractional compliance/client-account controller; acquisition/QoE CFO; local counsel; and one workflow engineer. Recruit through industry operators, incumbent alumni and specialist insurance recruiters; no claim that these people are already available or contracted.

Founder must learn commission recognition, renewal cohorts, carrier appointments, delegated authority, endorsements/exclusions, claims notification and limitation periods, premium accounting, E&O and conflicts. Publish a permissioned, anonymized benchmark with reviewer disagreement, failures and costs; appoint a named professional signatory; carry suitable cover; demonstrate a complete premium reconciliation. A polished AI demo is weak credibility compared with correct claim notifications and paid renewals.

Howden's documented employee share ownership is a precedent for giving producers economic participation (T7, transaction-notes.md), not a measured retention guarantee. Baldwin's contractual contingent consideration shows how deferred price can share risk (T8), but maximum payments and incentive conflicts belong in the model. Offer balanced seller rollover/earnouts tied to durable collected recurring revenue, client service and compliance, with enforceable local employment terms; counsel must assess restrictions. Do not automatically cut producer pay to manufacture an AI margin.

## Regulatory operating route
1. Retain licensed professional accountability during learning. Obtain data authority and signed service scope before file access. An unlicensed technology company supplies clerical tooling only within the perimeter counsel establishes.
2. Singapore: diligence existing registration and licence conditions; structure holding-company ownership with prior MAS effective-control approval at the applicable threshold and required appointments/other permissions. MAS toolkit is primary: https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/regulation/guidance/cmi/compliance-toolkit-for-insurance-brokers/compliance-toolkit-for-ibs-14-nov-2024.pdf . Registration processing expectations are not acquisition SLAs.
3. Hong Kong: obtain appropriate broker/company/representative/responsible-officer continuity; controller/director changes have a one-month post-change notification rule, distinct from Singapore prior control approval. IA guidance: https://www.ia.org.hk/en/legislative_framework/Conduct_in_Focus_Issue_08_05.html . Determine all transaction-specific conditions before signing.
4. Taiwan: use the current Chinese FSC rules, not stale English translation; qualified broker staffing, management changes, licensing, bonds/PI, premium remittance and ownership/foreign-investment questions need local counsel. https://law.fsc.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL027603 . Article 16 capital-adjustment trigger verified below; transaction-specific ownership permissions and foreign-investment timetable remain unverified; no unconditional Taiwan acquisition commitment.
5. Each entity has its own permissions and money controls; no automatic cross-border passport assumed. Do not move claims/client data to shared AI infrastructure until transfer, outsourcing, confidentiality and insurer contracts permit it. Obtain written professional advice before introducing percentage recovery fees or legal services.

## Capital and personal payout path
Stage 0 salary is an expense funded by capped founder/angel capital, not wealth creation. Anchor dividends come only from actual free corporate cash after tax, capital, debt, earnouts, integration and lawful distribution tests. Premium balances are not deployable capital.

Sponsor equity supplies acquisition purchasing power but dilutes founder common ownership. Negotiate common equity plus any management pool explicitly: investment amount, preference, hurdle, catch-up, promote participation, vesting, leaver treatment, dilution, rollover and secondary rights. A '10% management pool' is not '10% of enterprise value'. Founder can receive salary, lawful dividends, a sponsor-approved partial secondary, and eventual exit proceeds; none is guaranteed. The first acquisition's cash can be consumed by the next acquisition rather than distributed.

Illustrative USD1bn EV less USD300m net debt and USD20m fees leaves USD680m before preference/tax. At 3%/5%/10% genuinely participating common equity, proceeds are USD20.4m/34m/68m **only if no further preference reduces that pool**. A separate promote illustration returns USD400m sponsor capital/hurdle first; 15% of remaining USD280m to management, half to founder, yields USD21m. These are alternative waterfalls, not additive entitlements. A smaller event can pay something: USD300m common value ×12% founder ownership ×25% sale of the founder's holding =USD9m gross and 9% remaining common stake, assuming permission/liquidity.

Dividend recap is a new loan, not operating profit: hypothetical USD80m extra debt less USD10m fees/reserves distributes USD70m; a 10% common holder gets USD7m but the business adds USD8m annual interest at 10%, before taxes/amortization and constraints. Never assume lenders or regulators permit it. Personal target is USD20–30m **pre-tax unless explicitly grossed up**, with net personal cash requiring tax/jurisdiction advice. Minimum proceeds model required before accepting sponsor terms.

## What to prove in months 12–24
Show a complete renewal cycle, stable service and money controls before replicating. Widen APAC if the verified SG/HK/Taiwan available EBITDA pool cannot support the chosen scale. Add MGA/program business only with insurer capacity, delegated underwriting/pricing/governance and a profitable program cohort; add wholesale/reinsurance only with specialist talent and cedant/carrier demand. Neither removes underwriting-cycle or concentration risk. Source-backed comparator transactions show that strategic routes exist; they do not identify a guaranteed buyer at USD1bn.

Stop if expansion makes retention, compliance or debt capacity deteriorate. A profitable small broker with modest founder dividends is a valid operating result but does not establish an institutional exit. Keep the larger aspiration conditional on the ownership map, audited cash earnings and actual capital terms.

Coverage gate clarification: use cash available for debt service after cash tax, required capex, working-capital needs, earned seller payments and other senior cash obligations under the actual lender definition. EBITDA/(interest+principal) in the illustrative model is a separate diagnostic, not this cash covenant. Base simplified after-tax coverage is only1.539x before those additional uses; low1.034x fails.
