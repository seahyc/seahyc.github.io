## 1. Demand evidence: large adjacent behaviour, unknown paid wedge
### Anthropic personal guidance
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-personal-guidance (30 April 2026). Read the study's summary and sampling description. Ledger E01 holds the headline values and denominators. The unit and definition matter: unique-user-filtered conversations, seeking a personal course of action; not generic information. ## 3. What might justify a new product
The residual hypothesis is an evidence service attached to a recurring decision, not a celebrity directory. Candidate evidence object:
- original author/source URL, publication date, retrieved timestamp, locator;
- exact quotation if permitted, clearly separated paraphrase, local context and applicability conditions;
- contradictory source with its actual point of disagreement (values, constraints, time horizon, or empirical belief);
- dated public action and source, contemporaneous stated reasoning if available, unknowns and counterexamples;
- user-specific inference labelled as the host's/new synthesis;
- decision recorded before outcome, review date, observed result and alternative explanations.

These fields are proposed design, not evidence of a moat. Public “what they did” records are selected and often retrospective. No universal expert batting average is warranted: denominators, failed decisions, private constraints and counterfactual outcomes are missing. A prediction/outcome registry would require timestamping and coverage criteria before knowing results. A casebook with explicit unknowns is a more honest starting point. Even a correct action record need not reveal the cause of success or establish suitability for another founder.

## 5. Rights and licences: narrowly verified claims
Primary: court-authorized notice linked in E09, cover/Q15. The fund and release language are documented; no current final-order assertion is needed for the thesis. Its express absence of a future licence does not tell us whether another company's retrieval use is permitted. We have not assessed law across jurisdictions. Proposed launch policy: only material with documented permission/appropriate basis, no ambiguous full-book imports, minimum necessary excerpts, original-context preservation, user isolation, export/deletion and takedown path. These are design requirements for counsel/rights owners to validate, not a legal safe harbour.

Secondary roundup read: https://llmpulse.ai/blog/ai-content-licensing-deals/ . Its exhaustive-sounding table becomes qualified, dated wording in its FAQ. Treat it as a dated collection, not proof of no private or later agreements. Exclude “Anthropic has no publisher licensing deals” from a factual narrative. Positive evidence of Google publisher participation is sufficient to make the platform-competition point.
