# Provisional Needs Stack

Selected discovery candidate: communications runtime for agent-product builders. The user has accepted the infrastructure-company ambition, not this specific product choice. This stack is an analyst hypothesis based on the current request and prior research, not a completed customer workshop.

| Level, from means to end | Job | Real alternatives | Evidence / role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implementation | Connect authorized business accounts; synchronize history and inbound events; retain state through interruption | Direct platform APIs, Unipile, Nango, Composio, internal engineering | Current vendor capabilities observed; work the product would take over |
| Concrete product | Give the customer's own agent a continuous conversation stream and an approved action path | Existing integrations plus customer database, queues and workflow code; Beeper for personal contexts | Product hypothesis; several components already supplied competitively |
| Immediate outcome | Resume the right conversation after a new message, model change or interruption with less operator work | Humans checking inboxes, agency-built automation, existing messaging/CRM workflows | Plausible means-to-end connection; customer baseline and incremental benefit unmeasured |
| Business outcome | Handle more customer conversations without proportional staffing or missed follow-up | Hire operators; reduce coverage; buy a finished support/sales application | Prior marketplace records support related labor demand, not this API's willingness to pay |
| Aspiration | Grow customer revenue and service quality | Many unrelated growth and service investments | Do not guarantee; affected by message quality, offers and customer demand |

Suggested promise to test: “Your agent keeps the conversation going, even when you change models or restart the worker.” This is an unbuilt/unverified positioning hypothesis, not a present capability guarantee.
