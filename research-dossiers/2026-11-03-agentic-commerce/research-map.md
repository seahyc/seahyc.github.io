# Research map: consumer-fiduciary curation

3 November 2026. Founder section C controls the thesis.

**Founder decision:** Should a founder build a consumer-fiduciary research company for people delegating shopping to agents, beginning with US-delivered USB-C/Thunderbolt dock configurations through independent agent builders?

**Desired reader conclusion:** fund a capped experiment in checkable buyer value and paid retention; do not presume default routing, proprietary intent, membership demand or a venture-scale outcome. USB-C/Thunderbolt docks are a testable starting category, not validated market demand. Selection is the product: a maintained small shortlist, hard compatibility predicates, correct no-match, uncertain soft preferences and total-cost unknowns. Paying merchants cannot buy rank or entry. Consumer membership is the core willingness-to-pay hypothesis.

## Wedge choice and bear

Docks offer explicit host/display/cable/OS facts and auditable failures. Cables and chargers initially support configuration evidence, not separate catalogs. Running-shoe comfort and chair fit are harder cheap ground truths; supplements add health/evidence liability; generic electronics are too broad. These are screening judgments, not market-size rankings.

Strongest bear: consumer-interface owners control routing, catalogs, histories and contracts. Native research can improve; Keepa and independent labs can expose their own tools. Shopify's invite-led promoted-placement preview (E31) offers a native economic alternative to builders but is incompatible where payment influences selection. Its 0.3% documented rate is neither startup admission nor a valid membership revenue input. MCP may remain fragmented; a useful tool may be a free enrichment supplier.

Antonio García Martínez's walled-garden/programmatic-offer thesis (E35, practitioner opinion) is the central alternative world: consumer AI owners capture demand and advertising finances compute. His MCP emphasis supports the place to experiment, not our right to win. No-pay-to-rank is counter-positioning only if buyers value it, can verify it and voluntarily route/pay despite bundled native research. An incumbent's ad economics create a possible incentive conflict, not an inability theorem. Native organic/paid separation could be credible.

The wedge may simply be too infrequent for membership. Retained intent may be nonexclusive; outcomes too sparse or biased; lawful data and fixture maintenance too costly. Optional brand insight would introduce another conflict and cannot rescue the core forecast. Reject sponsored placement and commission-based exclusion even if those would make a different business profitable.

## Research and execution contract

Days 1–30: founder, hardware reviewer and engineer document 20 histories, 30 lawful tasks, two optional builder trials, source rights and shortlist criteria under $12k cumulative. Days 31–90: run benchmark, paid membership and quote tests under $45k cumulative. Month 6: optional routing, >=100 members, mature 90-day retention >=70% and positive measured contribution. Month 12: >=500 members plus independent outcome ablation. Month 24: three consecutive months operating break-even and separately financed/legal guarantee pilot. All numbers are proposed gates, not industry norms; immature cohorts delay decisions.

## Hypothesis-tree operational detail
Each row above separates documentary support from an unperformed test. Disconfirming evidence: native research already handles constraints (utility); client subregistries choose what to expose (routing); infrequent dock purchases challenge subscriptions (membership); incumbent organic-independence policies challenge novelty (neutrality); explicit catalog/review terms challenge reuse (rights); missing follow-ups and copied reviews challenge outcome learning; negative low-case contribution challenges volume economics. Missing evidence in all branches is actual rights, cohort behavior, benchmark performance and collected receipts. Founder owns payment and spending; builder owners own eligible-task denominators; hardware reviewer owns blinded labels; engineer owns full cost and latency; counsel owns source-use review.

**Next action:** founder + hardware reviewer, days 1–14, maximum USD5,000 within the USD45,000 cap: document 20 recent histories, at least 30 real tasks, source permissions and two builders willing to instrument optional trials. Go only if these are accessible and native answers leave a meaningful unresolved job. Otherwise stop or choose another wedge. No outreach or validation has occurred.
