# Execution: earn the next stage

3 November 2026. Proposed experiment, not completed customer work. Founding principle: consumer-fiduciary research, no pay-to-rank. Curate USB-C/Thunderbolt docks by buyer constraints, verified outcomes and true landed cost. Membership is the conservative core business to test; brand data and affiliate commissions fund none of the core model.

**Master Plan:** Curate one category better than anyone; use trust to win the agents; use their purchases to earn verified outcomes; use outcomes to guarantee purchases; use guarantees to become how agents buy.

Each stage earns rather than presumes the next. Membership receipts may offset costs; they are not assumed to fully fund expansion. Payments and guarantees require additional capital, partners and approval. This is an original proposed sequence, not a claim that a comparable company's growth economics transfer here.

## First 90 days: cash and people

Assumed ceiling $45,000: founder stipend $15,000; contract engineering $12,000; test devices $6,000; legal/privacy/licenses $5,000; data/compute $2,000; research operations $3,000; reserve $2,000. Do not buy inventory or hire a broad team before the rights/demand screen. Staged releases: $5k first 14 days, $12k cumulative by day 30, remainder only after lawful evidence and real optional trials exist. No external messaging, purchase or integration was performed by this research.

Founder's marketplace operating experience helps demand discovery and incentives; it does not establish hardware authority. Hire a contract USB-C/Thunderbolt reviewer with reproducible host/display fixtures and a technical lead able to separate deterministic compatibility from uncertain preferences. Add privacy/licensing counsel before retention or reuse. Finance ownership can remain with founder until receipts, refunds, cohort retention and actual labor allocations justify hiring.

Credibility comes from purchased test units where feasible, published protocols, exact model/firmware/OS fixtures, cited manufacturer facts, explicit unknowns, correction history and independent regrading. Permit brand factual appeals on the same evidence rules, with published corrections. Never charge for admission to the candidate set. Shortlist exclusion requires a buyer-fit/evidence/safety reason; lack of commission is not a reason.

## Buyers, distribution and willingness to pay

Buyer: individual developer or remote worker with a real host/display/dock decision; membership must solve repeat research, configuration upkeep or supported changes, rather than manufacture a recurring dock purchase. Agent builders are distribution partners, not presumed paying customers. Recruit only through authorized opted-in developer communities and builder channels; measure eligible-task denominators before attributing routing success to MCP discovery. Concierge trials should show the same candidates and method as the tool.

Offer a plainly priced, cancelable membership after showing useful research. Record offer exposure, actual payment, refunds, usage, cancellations and reasons. $5/$8/$10 scenarios are assumptions, not market-clearing prices. Test actual prices in separately reported cohorts without changing ranking; no invented interviews, preorders or buyer quotations. Stop membership expansion if fewer than 30 pay or fewer than 20 remain at 60 days after adequate offer exposure (at least 100 qualified users); a small immature cohort delays the conclusion. Consumer membership may fail despite excellent one-off utility.

## Economics and capital

`scenario-model.json` and `calculate-scenarios.py` are the canonical model; all low/base/high inputs are original assumptions. Monthly low/base/high operating contributions are -$10,212 / -$11,620 / +$30,500. Base: 2,000 members × [$8 − $3.81 variable/member] − $20,000 = -$11,620. Base break-even is 4,774 paying members if all unit costs/fixed costs remain unchanged. Low has negative contribution, so volume cannot rescue it; high break-even is 5,961 at its different assumptions.

Task costs include inference, data, testing QA and infrastructure; member costs include support, distribution, payment fees and churn replacement acquisition. Initial acquisition cash is separately $5k/$40k/$150k and growth CAC is additional: steady-state replacement does not finance growth. Two months of base spend without receipts is $55,240, already beyond the initial experiment budget. Do not mistake the positive high scenario for a financing plan. Taxes, financing and guarantee reserves are outside this research model; founder dividends come only after payroll, tax, reserves and reinvestment, never from gross receipts or fundraising.

## Legal, neutrality and stop rules

Rights gate: written source-use matrix for collection, processing, display, quotation, retention and cross-client outcome learning; no unlicensed source dependency. Use licensed feeds, manufacturer evidence or independently generated fixtures. The Comet appeal is not general commercial scraping clearance. Unknown price, shipping, tax, stock, loyalty benefit or variant must remain unknown. On 30 consenting checkout comparisons, >=90% must match within max($2,1%) for identical variant/destination/service, without buying; otherwise label estimates and fail the exact-cost promise. Never treat a model quote as authoritative checkout.

Publish ranking method and version; provenance and exclusion logs; revenue/ranking separation; consent and deletion; independent neutrality audit. Run counterfactual reranking with merchant revenue fields removed/perturbed: output must remain identical for identical buyer evidence. Membership buys service, never preference. Optional affiliate economics require uniform effective fee/rebate, nonpaying-candidate inclusion, written permission and firewall; otherwise revenue stays zero. Optional post-ranking insights require separate consent, proposed cohort >=30 distinct buyers, suppression of small/differencing cells, equal published prices and no ranking influence; legal review must determine if even that threshold is sufficient. Brand insight demand is unvalidated and excluded from financing.

No auctions, sponsored placement, catalog exclusion by commission or differentiated profitable-link optimization. If impartiality cannot survive without those, stop this thesis rather than rename paid steering. If optional invocation, hard-error reduction, transparent quote unknowns, paid retention or rights fail, stop or change the wedge before spending toward the next stage.

## Curation operating contract and Master Plan precedents
Initial candidate universe: lawfully documented manufacturer/retailer variants plus independently tested and buyer-nominated alternatives. Nonpaying products remain eligible; coverage gaps and unsupported variants are visible. Exact host/OS/display/cable/driver predicates precede soft buyer tradeoffs. Vendor facts, independent fixtures and confirmed owner experience remain different evidence classes. A maintained small shortlist can end in abstention or no purchase. Proposed cadence: stock/quote per request, source/compatibility review weekly, immediate review on material OS/firmware change or credible failure, independent retest when changed claims require it. These are unvalidated operating assumptions; measure maintenance cost and do not claim exhaustive testing. Brands may submit or appeal facts free under equal published rules, never buy placement or suppression.

Founder's five-stage summary: Curate one category better than anyone; use trust to win the agents; use their purchases to earn verified outcomes; use outcomes to guarantee purchases; use guarantees to become how agents buy. Tesla’s [original staged plan](https://www.tesla.com/secret-master-plan) is E47 structural precedent only; it does not validate these finances. E48 Trader Joe’s product selection and E41 Costco limited assortment show deliberate retail curation, not fiduciary equivalence. E49 AWS Working Backwards is the method for pr-faq.md; all launch dates, availability and quotes there are fictional scenarios. Stage one’s capped funds and member receipts do not presume affiliate income. Stage two expansion requires measured contribution and renewed demand. Stage three insights remain excluded. Stage four requires separately funded claims reserves and partner legal/control work. Opening the method/outcome schema at stage five offers interoperability under equal terms; adoption is unproven and incumbent independence policies remain counterevidence to an inability-to-copy claim.
