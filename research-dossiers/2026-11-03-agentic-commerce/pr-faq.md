# Working backwards: a shortlist that works for the buyer

**Hypothetical planning document, prepared 3 November 2026.** “Criteria” is an illustrative working name. The entire launch below is imagined, not an announcement, forecast or completed product. The date and both quotations are fictional. Features are proposed requirements; external facts carry ledger references. [S01/H01–03](evidence-ledger.md), [execution gates](execution-path.md).

Method: a future press release followed by customer and internal questions, following [AWS’s owning description of Working Backwards (E49)](https://aws.amazon.com/executive-insights/content/product-management-at-amazon/). This is a planning exercise, not validated customer evidence.

## Future press release — approximately one page

**Criteria introduces a curated dock shortlist your shopping agent can explain**

*An evidence-backed choice for a particular laptop and desk, with no paid ranking.*

**SINGAPORE — 15 February 2027 (fictional stage-one launch date, conditional on the gates below).** Criteria today introduces a research service for US shoppers choosing a USB-C or Thunderbolt dock. Instead of another long results page, it returns a small shortlist matched to the shopper's laptop, displays, charging needs, budget and willingness to install drivers. Every recommendation explains what fits, what was excluded and what remains unknown. This is the proposed first wedge, not a claim of validated demand. [H02](evidence-ledger.md)

A dock can look right and still miss a buyer's actual setup: Apple's published M2 MacBook Air specification, for example, supports one native external display. Manufacturer guidance and USB-IF documentation show why the exact host and cable matter. Criteria is designed to turn such constraints into a clear decision before someone orders. [E20–22](evidence-ledger.md)

The proposed experience starts with a few questions. Hard requirements eliminate incompatible candidates; preferences order the remaining choices. The service would show the best-supported few, or say no sufficiently evidenced match exists. Its category team would maintain a public selection method, dated source and fixture records, known limitations and a correction history. Prices would identify the checked variant and destination; unverified shipping, tax, stock or durability would remain unknown. These are design promises to test, not measured superiority. [H01–02/S01; native-price limitations E29](evidence-ledger.md)

“I wanted to know which dock would run my actual desk, and why—not read twenty more promising descriptions,” says **an illustrative customer, a fictional composite, not testimony**. “Seeing the rejected options and the remaining uncertainty helped me decide what to check.”

Membership would buy research access and support; it would never buy a product's position. Brands could submit factual evidence through the same review process but could not pay for inclusion, priority or removal of an unfavorable result. The core funding experiment is membership only. Affiliate fees and brand insights are excluded from core revenue. [H03/S01](evidence-ledger.md)

“Our job is to earn the right to make a smaller recommendation,” says **the illustrative founder in a fictional quotation**. “If someone can pay to change the answer, we have broken the product.”

At this imagined launch, shoppers would enter their setup in Criteria or ask a participating agent to use it. Builders would receive a documented API/MCP tool and example requests. Availability would be limited to explicitly tested integrations; shoppers using other agents could bring the resulting evidence report into their conversation. No universal agent support, automatic buying or purchase guarantee is promised. [Proposed scope H02/S01; registry limits E15](evidence-ledger.md)

## Customer FAQ — proposed launch policy

**How are picks chosen?** Start with a bounded candidate set, apply hard compatibility constraints, assess preferences and total cost, then select the best-supported few. Publish why candidates enter or leave it. Evidence quality and unknowns must be visible; a narrow shortlist cannot imply the whole market was exhaustively tested. Manufacturer facts are inputs, not independent reliability tests. [H01–02; E20–24](evidence-ledger.md)

**Why can't a brand pay to rank?** Revenue fields must be unavailable to the ranking process. Publish the method/version and exclusion reasons; test that changing hypothetical merchant payments leaves the result unchanged. These are proposed controls, not an already completed neutrality audit. Membership cannot change product preference. [S01](evidence-ledger.md)

**How do you make money?** Test a cancelable consumer membership. The $5/$8/$10 monthly model cases are assumptions, not announced prices or observed willingness to pay. A later uniform fee/rebate or consented insight product would need separate validation and a ranking firewall; neither funds this launch model. [H03/S01](evidence-ledger.md)

**What if the pick is wrong?** Show how to report the exact setup and failure, investigate, correct the record and notify affected consenting users. At stage one, the merchant's disclosed return/warranty terms remain relevant; Criteria proposes no reimbursement or compatibility guarantee. A guarantee comes only after separate underwriting, reserves and payment controls pass later gates. [S01; execution-path.md](execution-path.md)

**What happens to my information?** Collect only the setup needed for the answer. Retaining receipt evidence or follow-ups would require explicit consent and deletion controls; no raw chat resale. Removing a name alone is not proven anonymisation. [Proposed controls S01; E30](evidence-ledger.md)

**Which agents work?** Only those integrations tested and named at launch. MCP is a discoverability/interface mechanism, not evidence that all agents will call this tool. An ordinary report remains usable without a direct integration. [E15/H02](evidence-ledger.md)

**How can brands be considered?** Free factual submissions and appeals, reviewed against the published criteria. No purchase of admission, favorable coverage or suppression. Submissions can be declined for inadequate evidence; lack of commission cannot justify exclusion. [S01](evidence-ledger.md)

## Internal FAQ — questions that can stop the plan

**Why won't the incumbents do this?** They may. ChatGPT already gathers constraints and rejection feedback; OpenAI and Google already state organic-answer independence, and OpenAI describes checkout fees without product-ranking preference. Shopify provides broad discovery/checkout infrastructure. Better curation and observable evidence must win a matched test; “no ads” alone is no defensible moat. [E05–08/E29/E36–44](evidence-ledger.md)

**What must be true before launch and expansion?** The proposed 90-day gate is >=90% correctness among non-abstained tasks, >=80% coverage and >=30% fewer hard-error tasks than the strongest tested alternative on 100 held-out tasks; expand a baseline with fewer than 10 errors. Seek 30 actual paying members and >=20 still paying after 60 days in mature cohorts, after at least 100 qualified offers. These are decision rules, not results or statistical certainty. [S01; execution-path.md](execution-path.md)

**Does membership pay for the next stage?** Not yet. Assumed base: 2,000 members at $8/month, $3.81 variable cost/member including churn replacement acquisition, and $20,000 fixed cost produce **−$11,620/month**. Break-even is 4,774 members at unchanged costs; initial acquisition requires another $40,000. The 90-day cap is $45,000. Receipts offset costs, never automatically authorize expansion. [S01; scenario-model.json](scenario-model.json)

**How do agents find us?** Win optional use from unrelated builders, not registry listing alone. Month-six proposed gate: three integrations, two routing >=50% of eligible tasks for four weeks, with >=50 eligible tasks per qualifying app; 100 paying members, >=70% mature 90-day retention and positive measured contribution. Native tools can prevent distribution even if the answers are useful. [E15/H02/S01; execution-path.md](execution-path.md)

**Can we legally build the evidence base?** Only after field-by-field rights checks for collection, display, retention and learning. Shopify caching limits and RTINGS reuse restrictions prevent treating their content as a free owned corpus. The Comet appeal is not general scraping permission. Receipt verification is not proof a review is truthful; FTC rules prohibit specified fake reviews and sentiment-conditioned incentives, with disclosure duties separately relevant. [E06/E17–18/E24/E43](evidence-ledger.md)

**Why would curation scale?** Trader Joe’s describes a deliberate product-evaluation process, a selection precedent rather than proof of a neutral research business. [E48](https://www.traderjoes.com/content/dam/trjo/pdfs/transcripts/Inside-TJs-ICYMI-Tasting-Panel.pdf) Reuse the evidence schema, fixtures and correction process, but fund category expertise each time. The month-twelve gate asks whether consented outcomes reduce hard errors by >=20% against public-spec-only selection on fresh >=100 tasks without lower coverage. If outcomes add no value, no data-moat claim follows. [H01/S01; execution-path.md](execution-path.md)

**What is the master plan?** Curate one category better than anyone; use trust to win the agents; use their purchases to earn verified outcomes; use outcomes to guarantee purchases; use guarantees to become how agents buy. This is the founder's staged ambition, not achieved capability. [Tesla’s original August 2, 2006 plan (E47)](https://www.tesla.com/secret-master-plan) supplies the staged-reinvestment structure; its economics do not transfer. Membership is the sole modeled revenue; later checkout/guarantees need separate partners, financing, loss allocation and three months of operating break-even before the proposed pilot. Opening the method does not make platforms adopt it. [S01; execution-path.md](execution-path.md)

**What makes us stop?** Inadequate lawful evidence, weak measured advantage, failure of voluntary routing, poor paid retention, unsustainable contribution, or any need for paid steering. A missed maturity date delays judgment; a failed gate stops expansion or changes the wedge. No claim of exclusive outcomes, automatic category expansion or guaranteed purchases survives without evidence. [H01–03/S01; execution-path.md](execution-path.md)
