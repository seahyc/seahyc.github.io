# Mechanisms to evaluate explicitly in Phase 2

3 November 2026. Public evidence anchors use ledger IDs. Designs and tests below are original proposals, not claims these mechanisms work. This supplements the bounded dock wedge; it does not authorize transactions, outreach or purchases. Six mechanism cards can appear before the roughly one-third execution sequence; keep the final card count/visual coverage consistent.

## 1. Aggregate demand before transactions: can buying beat building?

The tool must earn a routing decision before a merchant sees the customer. A compatibility evidence service can help an agent already holding user demand; it cannot claim to own that demand merely because it is called. The proposed path is independent agent builders first, repeatable answers and permitted cross-client outcome learning second, then a supplier whose integration/QA cost exceeds its price to buy. Native ChatGPT research, Keepa's MCP, Shopify's catalog and client-curated registries (E05,E06,E15,E23) make this a competitive claim.

Counter-positioning hypothesis: publish hard failures, recommend a competitor or no purchase when appropriate, and price intelligence independently of placement. A retailer monetizing a bounded inventory may face a different incentive; this is an incentive hypothesis, not an assertion that incumbents cannot be honest. RTINGS already describes editorial independence, so impartiality itself is not novel. Buying beats building only if replacement would recreate permissioned fixtures, tested compatibility edges, freshness and correction history at greater cost. Measure that with partner renewal and ablation, not speculative acquisition interest.

**Falsifier:** independent builders can reproduce equal answers with native tools/public specs at lower full cost, or routing depends entirely on a forced instruction. A small niche service may remain useful; the default-tool/network thesis then fails.

## 2. Own structured intent early: hard and soft are different data

Hard constraints are predicates: host model, native external-display count, OS, minimum required port capabilities, budget and destination. Missing inputs generate a question. Soft preferences—portability, appearance, acceptable driver installation, service/warranty preference—are uncertain utility weights that the user can correct. Do not infer a health attribute, household identity or sensitive intent just to enrich analytics.

An exclusion record should distinguish hard incompatibility, evidence missing, stale stock/quote, user preference, budget and no decision. Agent rejection is not necessarily human rejection: retain actor/source and whether the user confirmed it. Record candidate exposure too; a product never considered was not rejected. A reason graph without those denominators is a misleading analytics product.

AEO/visibility tools try to influence or measure what answer engines cite. Impact's AI offering and CJ's AI visibility announcement are documented competitors. Our proposed product measures real eligible tasks and source-grounded exclusions; synthetic brand mentions are a separate metric. AEO vendors can add task telemetry, and platforms already capture intent/rejection (E05). Earliest access is not exclusivity. Retain only data with explicit purpose/rights and test whether it improves later answers (H01); do not sell raw conversations.

**Falsifier:** exclusions are mostly model errors, duplicated public information or unrepresentative client traffic; the retained graph adds no prospective improvement or paying decision value.

## 3. Which marketplace mechanisms carry over?

| Mechanism | What survives | What changes / design choice | Evidence or missing proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ranking | Candidate generation, constraints, tradeoffs | Hard filters precede preferences; explain unknowns and no-match; no sponsorship, auction or paid preference is permitted | Apple/USB-IF/CalDigit constraints (E20–22); utility needs test |
| Reputation | Product/seller reliability and dispute history | De-duplicate copied reviews; separate real experience, lab test and manufacturer claim; display exposure/time denominators | FTC deception rules, RTINGS precedent (E18,E25); no verified broad durability consensus |
| Offers/promotions | Merchants may provide unconditional price quotes | Compare true landed cost on equal access terms; no bids for recommendation access, sponsored placement or paid labels used as ranking inputs | Shopify paid-placement preview E31 is concrete incentive infrastructure; reverse auction remains proposed |
| Subscriptions | Consumer research membership | Membership buys research access, never preference; explicit renewal/cancellation; repeat dock value must be proved | Native payment protocols support recurring directions; no demand for our consumer subscription established |
| Guarantees | Allocate error/return downside | A correctness guarantee needs scope, partner pricing, funded reserve and claims process; don't promise universal durability or merchant performance | No insurer/guarantee quote; excluded from launch |
| Take rate | Monetize measurable incremental outcome | Optional only with uniform effective fee/rebate and ranking firewall; no commission-based candidate exclusion; zero in core model | Impact/CJ/Rakuten terms E26–28 and native Shopify E31 |

Agents can ignore irrelevant banners yet still consume paid placement, biased structured attributes, cherry-picked reviews and personalized prices. They are not automatically objective. This business prohibits recommendation auctions and paid placement. Merchants can submit factual corrections and equal-access net quotes; no payment buys inclusion, visibility or rank. Quote improvement may affect buyer cost, but merchant payment to the research utility never does.

Public practitioner opinion: Antonio García Martínez's [April 2 post](https://x.com/antoniogm/status/2039652629874164019) predicts seller bids and personalized offers behaving like ads for agents. His [March 22 article](https://x.com/i/article/2035723548405227520) argues that consumer-interface owners may capture value despite open protocols, and human enjoyment/status remain part of shopping. Root read both originals through Dia. These are opinions, not market data or proofs of future winners. The article's ACP/MPP versus UCP/x402 interpretation should not be restated as protocol fact: Stripe's own MPP announcement supports multiple methods, and ACP launch describes processor flexibility (E08,E32).

**Falsifier:** a private-offer experiment raises merchant yield but worsens buyer total cost or accepted fit; reject it even if it improves monetization.

## 4. Later transactions: own orchestration, use established rails

The credible option is an authorization/decision layer and merchant partnership, not a new payment network. Compare three routes only after research quality and legal demand are established:

| Route | Existing capability | Founder still must solve |
|---|---|---|
| ACP/UCP merchant checkout with AP2/credential support | Catalog/cart/merchant role and mandate interfaces (E08–10) | Binding exact variant, delivery and total to approval; timeout/retry/idempotency; disputes/returns and source rights |
| Virtual card through an issuing partner | Stripe documents amount/category/country controls; merchant-ID control is private preview (E34). Muse documents disposable Link credentials (E19) | Program/country eligibility, card lifecycle, one-use enforcement, exact merchant match, capture changes, fraud losses; a virtual number alone is not a one-use SKU guarantee |
| MPP/x402 to buy research/API resources | Stripe/Tempo MPP, Coinbase-origin x402; x402 now foundation-governed (E32–33) | Funding/spend limits, trusted payee, resource acceptance, retries/refunds and willingness to pay; API micropayments do not solve goods delivery |

A proposed passkey/biometric approval should display and bind to amount ceiling, payee, product/variant and expiry in a trusted interface. This is an architecture requirement to validate with partners, not a claim any documented card API already enforces every field. Keep secrets outside model context. A genuine approval of the wrong merchant or wrong product remains a failure.

Threats: prompt injection changing payee or basket; stale/poisoned evidence; unauthorized or replayed spend; simultaneous agents exceeding a budget; merchant category/ID mismatch; later capture/tips/fees; merchant nonfulfillment and refund abuse. Stripe explicitly warns spending aggregation can lag and later charges can exceed a limit (E34). Controls reduce some exposure, never establish fraud elimination. Quote/order agreement, bounded credentials, authoritative authorization and reconciliation need separate tests. Defer purchase capability if responsibility or unit reserves cannot be contracted.

## 5. Post-purchase experience: a receipt is only the first link

A proposed flow: opt-in proof-of-purchase token → consented agent reminder at days 30/60/90 → user-confirmed experience → evidence record linked to exact variant/configuration and elapsed exposure. Those days are illustrative follow-up choices. A receipt proves a purchase, not delivery, use, independence or durability. A model must not invent experience on behalf of a silent user.

Measure respondents and nonrespondents, cancellations, returns, use intensity and product age. Compare outcomes with pre-purchase constraints: did both monitors work, were drivers acceptable, what failed and when? Buyer can decline or delete. Report attrition and selection; do not infer failure rates from self-selected complaint counts.

Incentives: user gets better future advice and a personal warranty/returns record; builder gets useful, consented outcomes and agreed economics; merchant cannot buy praise or suppress negative evidence; any optional funding must have equal published terms and no access to ranking decisions. Any optional reward must not depend on sentiment and needs disclosure/terms review (E18). Proof tokens should avoid exposing order identifiers/payment data across clients. This loop can create useful evidence only if consent, sufficient follow-up and independent rights survive.

**Falsifier:** biased attrition, fabricated experience, unverifiable variants or too few longitudinal responses leave no measurable advantage over public specs. Do not claim durability verification then.

## 6. Align the players without hiding the conflict

| Player | Proposed benefit / commitment | Conflict or party disrupted |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer | Correct fit, visible total, unbiased no-purchase option; consent to minimal task processing | Data retention can undermine trust; sponsored steering is prohibited |
| Agent builder | Reliable small tool, lower QA burden, optional fixed, disclosed integration compensation with no product-ranking influence | Can build internally, route around us or demand all economics |
| Merchant/brand | Qualified configurations; optional post-ranking consented aggregate intelligence at an equal published price | Losing brands may dislike negative evidence or demand placement control |
| Marketplace | Better qualified demand and fewer wrong-fit orders, through permitted routes | Loses discovery/ad control; can restrict access or offer native research |
| Payment network/PSP | Incremental authorized volume using existing rails | Captures payment margin; research supplier does not earn network economics |
| Affiliate network/publisher | Valid attributable referral and funded licensed evidence | Zero-click answers remove traffic; subnetworks and automation may be restricted |
| Ad platform / AEO vendor | Lawful distribution; no recommendation auction or sponsored placement | Research tool threatens some discovery spend but paid machine offers remain ads |

No design makes every participant win on every task. Buyer-first ranking means some brands receive fewer purchases and some ad spend becomes less effective. Long-term credibility requires choosing the buyer's constraints when incentives conflict. The network effect is only a hypothesis: more **permissioned, independently informative outcomes** must improve answer quality across clients and sustain retention. More requests without transferable rights or outcomes are volume, not a moat.

**Expansion gate:** independently demonstrate the H5/H01 prospective ablation gain, retain unrelated client demand and positive measured contribution before entering transaction orchestration or promising a category-wide network. A decacorn outcome would need several such expansions and defensible distribution; it is not an arithmetic consequence of this wedge.

## Founding trust contract and economics

Parent-notes section C is controlling: ranking uses only buyer hard/soft fit, verified outcomes and true landed cost. No sponsored placement, reverse ad auction, pay-to-rank or commission-based catalog exclusion. Counter-positioning is an incentive hypothesis: ad-funded incumbents may face revenue sacrifice to adopt the same promise, but can still improve organic research. Antonio's June 2025 MCP sponsorship and August 2026 targeting/attribution views (E35) strengthen the bear: a tool interface alone does not remove advertising incentives. His non-fungible-shopping objection is less direct for this spec-heavy dock test, without proving demand.

Revenue test “can anyone pay to rank?”: membership passes only when it buys service/access without altering candidates or order. Optional affiliate passes only with uniform effective fee or rebate, nonpaying product inclusion and a separate settlement system with no ranking inputs; differentiated profitable links fail. Optional post-ranking insight passes only with explicit separate consent, proposed minimum cohort 30 distinct buyers, protection against small-cell/differencing identification, equal published pricing and no merchant ranking influence. Cohort threshold is a proposed control requiring legal review, not an anonymity guarantee. Brand insight and affiliate revenue are unvalidated and excluded from all core scenarios. Sponsored placement/auctions fail and are prohibited.

Publish candidate rules and ranking versions. Maintain source, freshness, uncertainty, exposure and exclusion records; no-purchase must remain a valid answer. Separate revenue credentials, tables and staff permissions from ranking inputs. Independently perturb/remove revenue fields and assert identical rankings given identical buyer evidence. Brands appeal factual errors on equal terms, never pay for shortlist inclusion. Publish corrections and permit privacy-preserving agent/consumer audit of a decision. No unqualified neutrality or durability claim before these tests.
