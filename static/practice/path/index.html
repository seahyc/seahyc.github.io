Championship standard

Frontier interview qualification

  1. Technique: worked pattern and focused variation
  2. Selection: mixed problems without a named method
  3. Contest: fresh timed work, no hints or pauses
  4. Proof: score correctness, systems judgment, testing and explanation
  5. Repair: classify the miss, solve a sibling, retrieve it after a gap

    Qualification requires blind coding, live changes, unfamiliar-code repair, role depth, a long-form competition event, and a five-hour peer-reviewed loop. Public tests are practice evidence; use a peer or private harness for held-out checks.
