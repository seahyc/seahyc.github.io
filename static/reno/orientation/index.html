Seasonal wind fit

Singapore's prevailing winds shift through the year. A good bearing creates opportunity, not a guarantee: neighbouring towers, rain screens, open doors and fan use still decide the indoor path.

December–MarchNE ↙

Northeast monsoon

North to northeast winds align best with the bedroom/study facade. Open interior doors and give air an exit at the living or yard side.

June–SeptemberSE ↖

Southwest monsoon period

Singapore commonly receives southerly to southeasterly flow. The southeast exposure and yard route become more useful as inlets.

April–May · October–November↝

Inter-monsoon

Winds are lighter and more variable. Ceiling fans and keeping a genuine inlet–outlet pair open matter more than compass alignment alone.

Purifier decision

Buy one movable HEPA unit, not a fleet.

The depot-facing exposure makes a backup more worthwhile, but it does not make an open-window purifier effective. Treat HEPA as a closed-room particle-control mode and use measured PM2.5—not orientation alone—to decide when to close up.

Windows open · normal air

Purifier off. Use natural cross-flow and fans. If depot-side noise or measured particles are troublesome, change modes: close that facade and run HEPA in the occupied room.

Haze / elevated PM2.5

Close windows, seal the room reasonably, then run HEPA continuously at a suitable clean-air delivery rate.

Closed air-conditioned bedroom

Move the purifier there if allergies, dust or sleep-time particle control justify it.

Cooking / renovation dust

Capture at source and ventilate first. HEPA helps with particles; it does not replace kitchen exhaust or remove all gases and odours.
