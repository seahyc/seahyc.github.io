In a capital cycle, a boom draws capital in, the capital builds capacity, capacity outruns demand and prices fall. The skill is telling, early, whether new capital will earn a return or only add supply.

**Numbers every case carries:** capex against operating cash flow, capex against depreciation, revenue per dollar of fixed assets, return on invested capital, industry capacity added against demand growth.
