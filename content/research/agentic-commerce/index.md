# THE BUYER'S AGENT

<div style="grid-column:1 / -1;width:100%;min-width:0;">
<p style="font-size:max(17px,1rem);line-height:1.58;"><strong>A founder’s Fieldbook · 3 November 2026 · Seah Ying Cong</strong></p>
<p style="font-size:max(17px,1rem);line-height:1.58;">Imagine telling your assistant what you need, and receiving a small set of things that actually fit your life. The dock works with your laptop. The apparent bargain includes delivery and tax. The recommendation can explain what it rejected, what it knows, and what remains uncertain.</p>
<figure style="max-width:600px;margin:1rem auto;"><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/buyer-guide.png" alt="A shopper and a research assistant examine compatible docks while filtering out a mismatched plug." style="width:100%;height:auto;background:transparent;"><figcaption>The buyer’s requirements determine the shortlist. Generated watercolor metaphor; no working product is implied.</figcaption></figure>
<p style="font-size:max(17px,1rem);line-height:1.58;">I want a research layer whose founding rule is simple: <strong>surface the best products for the consumer. Nobody can buy a better rank, and no retailer owns the boundary of the search.</strong></p>
<p style="font-size:max(17px,1rem);line-height:1.58;"><strong>My decision:</strong> test that company in dock compatibility, through independent agent builders. Charge buyers for useful research before assuming that merchant revenue will fund it. The ambitious destination is the trusted research tool agents choose first; the first obligation is to earn that choice.</p>
</div>

## 01 · How we choose

### Curate a shortlist, then fit it precisely to the buyer

I would begin with a maintained, opinionated set of docks, not an exhaustive search engine. The initial eligible universe comes from lawful manufacturer and retailer facts, independent fixtures and buyer-nominated alternatives. Paying nothing must never disqualify a product. Every coverage gap is visible.

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/selection.svg" alt="Eligible products pass hard predicates and evidence review into a shortlist, then undergo freshness checks, outcome learning and equal factual appeals." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;"><figcaption>Proposed selection and maintenance process. The cadence below is an operating proposal, not a completed testing programme.</figcaption></figure>

For each exact variant, check host, operating system, displays, cables and driver policy. Hard requirements are predicates, not preferences with large weights. Keep **pass / fail / unknown** separate. Then compare feasible candidates on the buyer’s softer priorities: portability, appearance, warranty, price and the strength of reliability evidence. An agent makes a curated set more useful by applying these rules precisely to each person.

A current total means product price plus mandatory shipping, tax and fees, less an eligible discount. Missing components stay estimated or unknown. Reject a candidate for an evidenced incompatibility, inadequate evidence or a buyer requirement—not for refusing a commission. Sometimes the result is a question, no match or no purchase.

My proposed maintenance cadence is live stock and quote checks per request, weekly compatibility-source review, immediate review on firmware/OS changes or a credible failure report, and periodic independent retesting when changed claims require it. Brands can appeal a fact under the same published evidence rules, without paying. This is an original policy to validate against actual cost and freshness needs.

ChatGPT already elicits requirements and rejection feedback. Our shortlist must prove better evidence and maintenance, not merely reproduce the conversation. [OpenAI shopping research](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-shopping-research/)

## 02 · Begin with something that can be wrong

### A dock cannot manufacture a capability the host does not have

My starting wedge is **USB-C/Thunderbolt docks for US-delivered laptop-and-display configurations**, reached through developer-oriented assistants. This is a founder hypothesis about testability and access, not a finding that docks are the largest market.

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/plug-reality.png" alt="A laptop drives one display while another remains disconnected beside a many-port dock." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;background:transparent;"><figcaption>More sockets do not establish a compatible display path. Illustrative arrangement, not a test of the depicted hardware.</figcaption></figure>

Apple specifies **one native external display, up to 6K at 60Hz**, for the M2 MacBook Air. That is a host constraint, not proof that every driver-mediated alternative is impossible. The assistant must ask whether installing such software is acceptable. CalDigit’s compatibility guidance similarly distinguishes hosts and display paths. [Apple specifications](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/111867) · [CalDigit guidance](https://www.caldigit.com/caldigit-docks-and-apple-m1-pro-m1-max-compatibility/)

I like this wedge because the wrong answer can be identified. Port shape, transport capability, host support and cable capability are separate facts. USB-IF’s capability and certification materials provide useful evidence classes; they do not certify general durability. [USB-IF cable guidance](https://www.usb.org/cable_connector)

The unattractive part is equally clear: a person may need this help only occasionally. A useful dock answer does not establish a subscription business.

## 03 · Demand is moving; our demand is unproven

### Follow the shopper without inventing a market size

Adobe observes purchases following AI referrals to retailers. Worldpay surveys willingness to use AI for shopping. These are reasons to investigate changing behavior. Neither tells me how many people will pay an independent compatibility researcher. [Adobe retail analysis](https://business.adobe.com/blog/ai-traffic-higher-revenue-per-visit-retail-holiday) · [Worldpay report article](https://worldpay.com/en/insights/articles/agentic-commerce-report-2026-out-now)

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/demand.svg" alt="Survey interest, retail referral, improved decision and paid renewal are four different evidence classes." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;"><figcaption>No funnel conversion is inferred between these observations. The bottom stages require our own experiment.</figcaption></figure>

Adobe’s channel comparison is observational: people arriving from AI may differ from other visitors. A referral is not autonomous purchasing. Worldpay’s payment attitudes have a filtered denominator of people already open to AI, and the article’s publication metadata does not establish survey fieldwork dates. I would not multiply those figures into a startup revenue forecast.

The demand question is smaller and harder: can we observe an actual buying task, improve on the strongest available answer, and persuade the person to fund continued help? That is the bridge from an interesting distribution shift to a company.

## 04 · The research layer is already occupied

### Every part of the stack has a serious incumbent

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/competition.svg" alt="Consumer interfaces, catalogs, evidence providers and payment systems already have suppliers." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;"><figcaption>Documented propositions at the research cutoff. This is a map of competition, not a hands-on capability score.</figcaption></figure>

OpenAI offers research and checkout. Perplexity offers a shopping journey. Amazon’s Buy for Me reaches beyond its own store; its shopping assistant has also evolved. The breadth matters even when product pages do not establish reliable execution on our task. [OpenAI checkout](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/) · [Perplexity shopping](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/shopping-that-puts-you-first) · [Amazon Buy for Me](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-shopping-app-buy-for-me-brands) · [Amazon shopping assistant](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-agentic-ai-gen-ai-shopping)

Shopify supplies agent-facing catalogs through UCP and MCP. Keepa already exposes product and price-history research through an official MCP service backed by its paid API. A founder cannot honestly sell “shopping data through a tool” as untouched territory. [Shopify Catalog](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/catalog) · [Keepa MCP](https://keepa.com/api-docs/mcp.html)

Meta’s Muse and xAI’s Grok Bot broaden the set of agents that can act on the web; their existence does not prove shopping volume or access to every retailer. [Meta announcement](https://about.fb.com/ja/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/) · [xAI announcement](https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot)

Shopify also documents an invite-led promoted-placement program, with attribution and settlement restrictions. That gives builders a native revenue alternative; it does not authorize our participation or make payment-influenced selection compatible with our promise. [Shopify promoted placements](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/catalog/promoted-placement)

My proposed place is narrower: independently maintained configuration evidence that several clients prefer to rebuilding themselves.

## 05 · Independence must survive the revenue meeting

### No pay-to-rank is an operating constraint

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/revenue-wall.png" alt="A wall separates a merchant offering money from the assistant evaluating products for a buyer." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;background:transparent;"><figcaption>Revenue cannot cross into the selection decision. Generated metaphor for a proposed organizational and technical boundary.</figcaption></figure>

The attractive counter-position is to refuse the auction: buyer fit, verified outcomes and true landed cost determine rank. A merchant cannot pay for admission, preference, the removal of an unfavorable finding, or access to a private conversation. “Consumer-fiduciary” describes that proposed duty of loyalty; I am not asserting a legally established fiduciary status.

But the easy competitive story would be false. ChatGPT has ads, while OpenAI explicitly says advertising does not influence answers. Its shopping help separately says product results are independent of partnerships. Google’s Search AI experiences carry ads and also state that paid advertising does not influence organic AI responses. [OpenAI ads policy](https://openai.com/index/expanding-access-to-ai-with-chatgpt-ads/) · [Shopping selection policy](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search) · [Google Search policy](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/18826097)

Google Search AI Mode is not the Gemini app. A dated Alphabet statement about the app’s free tier and subscriptions does not settle its current ad status. Perplexity’s original ad experiment promised answer independence; fresh primary evidence did not establish its current selling status here. [Alphabet statement](https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_events/2026/Apr/29/2026_Q1_Earnings_Transcript.pdf) · [Perplexity’s historical policy](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/why-we-re-experimenting-with-advertising)

My inference is an incentive conflict worth testing, not an inability theorem. A startup earns an advantage only if buyers can verify its stronger commitment and value it enough to choose it.

## 06 · People already pay for judgment

### Borrow the discipline, not the economics

Consumer Reports provides an encouraging precedent: a nonprofit supported by members alongside donors, philanthropy and partnerships. Its commercial-partner policy allows fees while explicitly rejecting purchased placement, reviews or ratings. Independence is a governance practice, not simply the absence of business relationships. [Funding](https://www.consumerreports.org/cro/about-us/our-partners/index.htm) · [Commercial policy](https://www.consumerreports.org/about-us/partners-and-major-gifts/commercial-partners/)

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/precedents.svg" alt="Costco fiscal 2026 membership-fee revenue was 5.907 billion dollars and whole-company net income 9.226 billion dollars; distinct accounting measures." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;"><figcaption>Reconstructed from Costco’s filing. Fee revenue is not fee profit; these bars do not allocate the company’s costs.</figcaption></figure>

Costco’s **FY2026 membership-fee revenue was $5.907 billion**, alongside **$9.226 billion in whole-company net income**. The filing demonstrates substantial recurring fees within a retailer, not that every dollar of fee revenue becomes profit or that a research startup can reproduce its economics. [Costco Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983226000093/cost-20260830.htm)

Wirecutter’s owning-company help policy says editorial recommendations are independent of business deals and editors are kept unaware of retailer relationships. RTINGS describes purchased test units, a published method and separation of testing from revenue. These are close precedents—and potential competitors. [Wirecutter policy](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/21870729656596-Wirecutter-Product-Recommendations) · [RTINGS independence](https://www.rtings.com/company/how-we-make-money)

Costco also reports **fewer than 4,000 active warehouse SKUs**, compared with **9,000–11,000 online**. That is bounded selection in a real retailer, not proof the same shortlist suits every person. Trader Joe’s own tasting-panel account describes internal product evaluation before selection. I take these as precedents for judgment over abundance, not transferable algorithms. [Costco selection](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983226000093/cost-20260830.htm) · [Trader Joe’s selection process](https://www.traderjoes.com/content/dam/trjo/pdfs/transcripts/Inside-TJs-ICYMI-Tasting-Panel.pdf)

The lesson I take is that people can fund judgment. The missing evidence is whether our particular judgment earns recurring payment.

## 07 · Put every revenue line through the same test

### Can anyone pay to rank?

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/revenue.svg" alt="Membership is the core model; flat fees, affiliate rebates and aggregate insights are conditional; sponsored ranking is rejected." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;"><figcaption>Proposed commercial constitution. Conditional lines contribute nothing to the financial model.</figcaption></figure>

**Membership passes most cleanly.** The consumer pays for access to research, configuration upkeep or supported decisions. The selection method stays the same. Cancellation must be easy. The obvious failure is infrequent use: independence may be valuable while monthly membership is the wrong package.

**A flat take on completed purchases can pass conditionally.** The effective fee must be uniform, nonpaying products must remain eligible, and the shopper must see the charge. OpenAI’s checkout announcement is a narrow precedent for a seller fee with stated product-ranking independence; it does not prove uniform fees or observed neutrality. [Instant Checkout policy](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/)

A seller may offer a lower net price to the buyer. Compare that quote under the same constraints and total-cost method; never auction visibility or let a bid purchase a place in the candidate set.

**Affiliate revenue needs a stronger firewall.** Rank before attaching monetized links; exclude commission fields from selection; rebate variable excess rather than favor the profitable link. Written program terms and actual settlement still matter. Impact, CJ and Rakuten document contract-specific qualification and attribution, not universal permission for agent traffic. [Impact contract fields](https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/reporting-for-partners/contract-history-reports/contract-history-detail-report) · [CJ terms caveat](https://docs.cj.com/docs/advertiserlookup-api) · [Rakuten policies](https://rakutenadvertising.com/legal-notices/affiliate-network-policies/)

**Post-ranking insights remain an option, not a subsidy assumption.** Only separately consented, protected aggregate evidence at published terms; no raw chats, purchased suppression or ranking influence. Even then, a brand customer creates pressure. If the business needs paid steering to survive, this thesis has failed.

## 08 · The strongest bear has the distribution

### García Martínez may be right about who captures demand

Antonio García Martínez’s central argument deserves to lead the bear case. In his public essay, open commerce protocols need not prevent the owner of the consumer AI experience from becoming the dominant aggregator. Human shopping also includes enjoyment, identity and persuasion; it is not all a computation over commodity specifications. [His essay and post](https://x.com/antoniogm/status/2035742973258825830) · [X article](https://x.com/i/article/2035723548405227520)

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/garden-gate.png" alt="An independent research assistant waits outside a garden whose gatekeeper controls access to shoppers and catalogs." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;background:transparent;"><figcaption>Useful external evidence can still be denied distribution. Editorial metaphor, not a claim about any platform’s specific policy.</figcaption></figure>

His other posts connect the mechanism: build at the MCP layer; sellers can bid through personalized programmatic offers; agents do not abolish the targeting, attribution and budgeting beneath advertising. These are practitioner opinions, recorded from author reads, not independently replicated market findings. [MCP view](https://x.com/antoniogm/status/1935420005014708248) · [Programmatic offers](https://x.com/antoniogm/status/1935721402952208555) · [Seller bids](https://x.com/antoniogm/status/2039652629874164019) · [Advertising continuity](https://x.com/antoniogm/status/2089369428412846372)

I agree that the interface owner can capture the economics. I choose a spec-driven, relatively fungible wedge precisely because it reduces the lifestyle objection. I reject paid ranking as the product I want to build, while accepting that an offer-funded competitor may distribute its service more cheaply.

Our counter-position wins only if a meaningful buyer segment wants auditable allegiance enough to route around the garden. Otherwise we are an enrichment supplier—or a small research business. An open endpoint does not change that bargaining position.

## 09 · Aggregate demand before executing purchases

### Be called first for a reason

MCP is a standard way for an agent to call a tool. The registry makes servers discoverable; client marketplaces can curate their own selections. It is neither a neutral search-ranking system nor proof that invocations concentrate in a winner. [Registry architecture](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2025-09-08-mcp-registry-preview/)

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/routing.svg" alt="Discoverability leads to configuration, optional invocation, useful answers, repeat use and buyer payment, each requiring separate evidence." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;"><figcaption>Proposed demand-first funnel. Count skipped eligible tasks as well as successful calls.</figcaption></figure>

I would expose a small read-only API and MCP tools for constraints, evidence and cost. The caller receives exact variants, failures, unknowns, sources and timestamps. A concise reliable result can save a builder retrieval and verification work without taking over its consumer interface.

The hoped-for acquisition logic is that replacing our fixtures, licenses and correction history eventually costs more than buying the service. That is a hypothesis to test through renewal and replaceability, never an exit plan inferred from traffic.

The AEO comparison here is deliberately limited to the CJ/Impact evidence in this investigation. Measuring or influencing answer visibility is different from observing a real eligible task and its outcome. It is not proof that this company owns intent, nor a complete survey of AEO competitors. [Impact AI direction](https://impact.com/ai/) · [CJ documentation](https://docs.cj.com/docs/advertiserlookup-api)

## 10 · Capture why; do not pretend it is exclusive

### An intent graph is useful only if it improves the next answer

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/intent.svg" alt="A useful rejection record includes exposure, configuration, reason, actor, evidence time and consented outcome before a prospective test." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;"><figcaption>Proposed evidence schema. Agent rejection, user rejection and factual incompatibility are different events.</figcaption></figure>

A structured request can arrive before any merchant sees the shopper. That timing is strategically interesting. Record the buyer’s confirmed hard constraints, uncertain preferences, candidate exposure, exclusion reasons and evidence state. A product never considered was not rejected. An agent’s mistaken exclusion is not consumer demand.

The potentially valuable asset is the relationship between configurations and later outcomes: what worked, what needed drivers, what failed after a change. Public specifications rarely answer every operational question. But early access confers no exclusivity. ChatGPT already collects rejection feedback; clients and users can retain the same history. [Native feedback flow](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-shopping-research/)

I would test the asset prospectively: compare answers using public specifications alone against answers with permissioned outcome evidence on fresh tasks. Preserve source rights and allow deletion. If the outcome layer adds no measurable information, retire the moat claim.

The ambition is a compounding evidence system. The obligation is to demonstrate the compounding rather than count conversations and call them a network effect.

## 11 · A receipt begins the review

### Reputation needs real experience and elapsed time

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/receipt-loop.png" alt="A dock, a purchase receipt and successive plant-growth stages lead to an assistant recording later experience." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;background:transparent;"><figcaption>Purchase → use over time → confirmed experience. A receipt alone proves none of the later steps.</figcaption></figure>

I would offer an opt-in post-purchase record: confirm the exact configuration, then ask through the user’s agent whether it worked and what changed. Follow-ups at **days 30, 60 and 90** are proposed collection intervals, not established durability thresholds. [Proposed outcome stage](#card-14-the-master-plan)

Record exposure time, returns, respondents and nonrespondents. Separate owner testimony, lab tests and manufacturer statements. Repeated copies of a review are not independent votes. An agent can summarize a person’s confirmed experience; it must not manufacture one for a silent user.

The user gains a useful configuration and warranty history; the builder gains better future answers. A merchant may help resolve a problem but cannot purchase praise or erase an unfavorable finding. Neutral collection incentives would still need disclosure and legal review.

The FTC’s **2024 rule**, effective **October 21**, addresses specified false reviews, suppression and sentiment-conditioned incentives. The Endorsement Guides address material connections and truthful endorsements; the Guides themselves are not the same legal instrument as the rule. Neither proves a fraud prevalence rate, and disclosure alone cannot make a biased ranking neutral. [FTC rule Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers) · [Endorsement Guides Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking)

## 12 · Own the decision before the payment

### The transaction layer is a later responsibility

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/payments.svg" alt="Research and quote precede trusted approval, partner execution and reconciliation of delivery, refunds and disputes." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;"><figcaption>Later architecture to validate. No purchase integration, card program or guarantee was tested.</figcaption></figure>

ACP and UCP provide merchant-journey interfaces. Google’s AP2 describes signed mandates. Visa and Mastercard describe agent trust and payment programs. Stripe/Tempo’s MPP and x402 address programmatic payments, including resources used by agents. These mechanisms do not establish recommendation truth or remove delivery and dispute obligations. [ACP](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-openai-instant-checkout) · [UCP](https://developers.googleblog.com/under-the-hood-universal-commerce-protocol-ucp/) · [AP2](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/announcing-agents-to-payments-ap2-protocol) · [Visa](https://corporate.visa.com/en/sites/visa-perspectives/newsroom/visa-unveils-trusted-agent-protocol-for-ai-commerce.html) · [Mastercard announcement](https://www.mastercard.com/global/en/news-and-trends/press/2025/april/mastercard-unveils-agent-pay-pioneering-agentic-payments-technology-to-power-commerce-in-the-age-of-ai.html) · [MPP](https://stripe.com/blog/machine-payments-protocol) · [x402](https://www.coinbase.com/developer-platform/discover/launches/x402)

A later service could bind a trusted passkey or biometric approval to the exact product, payee, amount ceiling and expiry, then use a partner’s rails. Virtual cards and network tokens can limit credential exposure. A disposable number is not automatically a single-use, exact-product authorization: Stripe’s documented spending controls have aggregation and later-charge caveats. [Issuing controls](https://docs.stripe.com/issuing/controls/spending-controls)

Prompt injection can corrupt a basket or payee; concurrent agents can exceed a budget; a merchant can fail to fulfill or abuse refunds. Authorization, reconciliation and loss allocation require separate proof. Agent identity is a related but different layer, covered in [Trust When Agents Arrive](/learning/trust-when-agents-arrive/).

I would use payment networks as suppliers. The strategic asset is the buyer’s approved decision and evidence record, if users choose to entrust it to us.

## 13 · The membership test

### Independence has a full cost

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/economics.svg" alt="Assumed low, base and high membership scenarios have monthly operating contributions of minus 10,212, minus 11,620 and plus 30,500 dollars." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;"><figcaption>Original USD scenarios. All prices, volumes and costs are assumptions; every case has zero merchant revenue. Joint favorable assumptions are not a forecast.</figcaption></figure>

My assumed base is **2,000 members paying $8 per month**. Allow **four tasks per member at $0.21 each**, **$0.75 support**, **$0.30 distribution**, **4% payment cost**, and replacement acquisition of **8% monthly churn at $20 per member**. That leaves **$4.19 contribution per member** before **$20,000 fixed monthly costs**: an operating loss of **$11,620**. [Original model](#card-22-evidence-and-stop-rules)

At unchanged unit and fixed costs, break-even is **4,774 members**. The low case has negative member contribution, so volume makes it worse. The high case assumes better retention, acquisition and delivery economics together; its surplus precedes tax, financing, guarantee reserves and distributions. [Model arithmetic and sensitivities](#card-22-evidence-and-stop-rules)

**What the headline omits:** research costs money without a purchase. Freshness, lawful data, human testing, failed calls, abstentions and support all count. Subscription churn can overwhelm cheap inference. Initial acquisition cash and growth acquisition are additional to steady-state replacement costs.

This model is a tool for discovering what must become true. It is not evidence that recurring dock research is worth the assumed membership price.

## 14 · The Master Plan

### Each stage proves something and earns the next

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/master-plan.svg" alt="Five proposed stages: curate a category, earn routing, collect useful outcomes, guarantee bounded purchases and open the standard." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;background:transparent;"><figcaption>Five proposed gates. Member receipts may offset costs; they are not assumed to finance expansion before positive contribution.</figcaption></figure>

**Curate one category better than anyone; use trust to win the agents; use their purchases to earn verified outcomes; use outcomes to guarantee purchases; use guarantees to become how agents buy.** That is the proposed Master Plan. It is a sequence of earned responsibilities, not an assertion that the intermediate assets already exist.

Tesla’s original Master Plan is the structural precedent: a staged product and funding sequence. Its manufacturing economics do not transfer to this company. [Tesla’s original post](https://www.tesla.com/secret-master-plan)

**Curation → routing.** Prove lawful evidence and useful selection, then paid membership. Founder capital funds the experiment; receipts offset it. Do not assume commissions.

**Routing → outcomes.** Earn optional calls, mature retention and positive contribution before another category. Reuse the method and adapters; repeat the benchmark and rights review. Membership funds only what collected contribution supports.

**Outcomes → guarantees.** Show that permissioned follow-ups improve later answers. Insight sales remain excluded from the model. A scoped guarantee needs separate pricing, partners and reserves, even if the data improves.

**Guarantees → an open standard.** Publish the method and outcome schema under equal integration terms. Make selection portable across agents and merchants. Adoption remains uncertain; incumbents already promise organic independence.

The proposed **12–24 month** programme and its numeric gates appear in the following stage cards and appendix. Keepa and RTINGS establish packaging and independent-testing precedents, not this growth sequence or its profitability. [Original gate assumptions](#card-22-evidence-and-stop-rules) · [Keepa](https://keepa.com/api-docs/mcp.html) · [RTINGS](https://www.rtings.com/company/how-we-make-money)

## 15 · Stage one: curate at a bench

### Spend against evidence, not category breadth

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/bench-work.png" alt="A hardware reviewer and researcher test a dock, cables and displays while recording results." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;background:transparent;"><figcaption>Proposed early work: reproduce configurations and document failures. No test units were operated for this report.</figcaption></figure>

In the **first 30 days**, the founder observes **20 recent purchase or support histories**, documents **30 lawful eligible tasks**, and seeks **two unrelated builders** willing to instrument optional trials. The hardware reviewer freezes exact host, OS, display and cable fixtures. Counsel checks permitted use of each required field. The cumulative spend ceiling is **$12,000**, inside the larger experiment cap. [Original experiment gates](#card-22-evidence-and-stop-rules)

During **days 31–90**, the engineer builds the bounded tool; an independent reviewer grades **100 held-out tasks** against native research, manufacturer guidance and the best accessible alternative. Require **90% correctness among answered tasks**, **80% coverage**, and **30% fewer hard-error tasks**. If the baseline has fewer than **10 errors**, expand the sample instead of celebrating a fragile relative improvement. [Pre-registered screening rules](#card-22-evidence-and-stop-rules)

Then expose at least **100 qualified users** to a clear paid offer. Seek **30 actual members**, with **20 still paying after 60 days**, delaying the retention verdict until the cohort matures. These are founder-set screening thresholds, not industry norms or observed results. [Membership gate](#card-22-evidence-and-stop-rules)

First receipts offset the experiment; they do not enlarge its cap. If the best existing answer is already sufficient, or subscriptions add no recurring value, a polished tool is not a reason to expand.

## 16 · Stage two: win optional routing

### Independent builders distribute; people decide whether to pay

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/distribution.svg" alt="Find independent builder owners, test optional selection, offer consumers a paid trial, then retain useful cohorts." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;"><figcaption>A proposed route to demand. No named prospect has committed to integrate or purchase.</figcaption></figure>

The first partner is a product or engineering owner at an independent personal assistant or hardware-selection agent. Reach these people through relevant developer communities, public tool-maintainer channels and founder introductions. Ask for their real task denominator and a bounded comparison, rather than a logo or guaranteed call instruction.

The consumer buyer is a developer or remote worker solving a real configuration problem. Ask for a plainly priced, cancelable membership after showing useful work. Configuration upkeep and later supported decisions must earn renewal; the product cannot manufacture another dock purchase every month.

My proposed **month-6 distribution gate** is **three unrelated integrations**, with **two builders routing at least 50% of eligible tasks** over **four weeks**, each with at least **50 eligible tasks**. Exclude founder calls and forced routing. Target **100 paying members**, **70% mature 90-day retention**, and positive measured contribution after churn replacement acquisition. [Original stage gates](#card-22-evidence-and-stop-rules)

Reliable schemas, concise answers, explicit unknowns and useful corrections may earn preference. A registry listing makes the experiment possible; it does not make the service the agents’ default. The API should remain usable without MCP. Only collected positive contribution can finance the next learning tranche; otherwise narrow scope or seek a separately justified funding commitment. [Registry design](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2025-09-08-mcp-registry-preview/)

## 17 · Stage three: earn the outcome asset

### Trust becomes a moat only through better future answers

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/outcomes.svg" alt="Permissioned purchases and follow-ups feed an independent comparison against public specifications before claiming a moat." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;"><figcaption>Proposed learning loop. Intervals, member counts and error-reduction thresholds are assumptions, not observed results.</figcaption></figure>

At the proposed **month-12 gate**, seek **500 paying members**, mature retention evidence and **20% fewer hard-error tasks on at least 100 fresh tasks** when outcome records are added to public specifications, with unchanged or better coverage and adequate baseline errors. Otherwise retire the data-moat claim. [Original stage rules](#card-22-evidence-and-stop-rules)

Members’ collected contribution may pay for the next evidence tranche. It is not guaranteed to cover it; the base model loses money. Optional aggregate insight sales require separate consent and privacy protections and contribute nothing to the forecast. The most dangerous shortcut is calling every retained conversation an exclusive asset.

A founder with marketplace operating experience should recruit the knowledge they lack: an engineer who separates deterministic rules from uncertain retrieval, a USB/Thunderbolt practitioner with reproducible fixtures, and privacy/licensing counsel. Test labs, hardware support communities and developer networks are recruiting routes, not committed relationships.

Publish supported scope, methods, source dates, exclusions and corrections. Keep candidate-generation and ranking logs. An independent reviewer should replay decisions after removing or perturbing merchant economics: the same buyer evidence must yield the same rank. Include nonpaying products and show coverage gaps. A perfect ranker over a commission-selected catalog still breaks the promise.

RTINGS and Consumer Reports provide operating precedents for published methods and commercial separation. They do not certify our implementation. [RTINGS policy](https://www.rtings.com/company/how-we-make-money) · [Consumer Reports policy](https://www.consumerreports.org/about-us/partners-and-major-gifts/commercial-partners/)

## 18 · Finance a useful company first

### Dividends require residual cash

Release spending by gate. Borrow hardware where permitted; narrow scope if rights or fixtures cost more. [Original budget](/images/agentic-commerce/capital.svg)

The model’s base **initial acquisition cash is $40,000**, additional to operating economics. [Original cash model](#card-22-evidence-and-stop-rules)

A stipend is compensation. Dividends come after company costs, tax, reserves and reinvestment. A secondary requires a buyer and agreed terms; an acquisition requires strategic demand and transferable rights. A decacorn path would require repeated expansion beyond docks. I would build a company capable of paying its way, then decide whether the evidence warrants that larger ambition.

## 19 · Guarantees, then an open standard

### Earn the liability before selling the promise

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/guarantee.svg" alt="Scoped approval and priced cover precede a reserved guarantee pilot, followed by an open method and interoperable records." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;"><figcaption>Proposed later stages. A useful recommendation does not establish priced insurance, purchase authorization or merchant loss allocation.</figcaption></figure>

The proposed **month-24 option** begins only after **three consecutive months of full operating break-even**, partner-priced cover, a stress-tested reserve and tested exact-approval/loss allocation. A guarantee might cover failure of a stated compatibility criterion; it must not quietly imply universal durability or fulfillment insurance. No guarantee revenue or claims costs are modeled. [Original conditional gates](#card-22-evidence-and-stop-rules)

Use collected surplus or separately raised capital for the reserve; membership alone does not make claims liability disappear. Only then consider an open fiduciary method and outcome protocol, with equal terms and no exclusivity. That may earn integration; it cannot force platforms to adopt it.

Before pilot ingestion, counsel must document permission for collection, display, retention and derived cross-client learning. Shopify’s catalog guidelines restrict caching; RTINGS restricts systematic commercial reuse. Link or license evidence, or generate it through our own fixtures. [Shopify guidelines](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/catalog) · [RTINGS terms](https://www.rtings.com/company/terms-of-use)

Amazon’s litigation against Perplexity is a warning against treating user direction as blanket access permission. The appellate opinion vacated a preliminary injunction on a limited access-law record and preserved private-contract questions. It did not grant unrestricted scraping or data resale; this research does not establish the complete later docket. [Appellate opinion](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2026/08/04/26-1444.pdf)

The buyer gets fit and an honest no-purchase option; the builder gets dependable evidence; a merchant gets better-qualified demand; publishers get permissioned licensing; payment networks retain execution volume. Some ad intermediaries lose steering revenue, and some merchants lose sales when evidence goes against them. These conflicts cannot all be harmonized.

Publish integration terms without exclusivity. Minimize retained user context, separate outcome consent, support deletion and export, and assess reidentification before any aggregate report. Anonymisation guidance is a starting point, not worldwide legal clearance. [PDPC resource](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/help-and-resources/2018/01/basic-anonymisation)

The open-market promise means searching beyond paying suppliers within disclosed lawful coverage. It cannot mean claiming omniscience or bypassing a source’s restrictions.

## 21 · Questions that can kill the plan

<figure><img src="/images/agentic-commerce/faq.svg" alt="Why this pick, who pays, what happens when wrong and why build are questions tied to evidence and stop conditions." style="max-width:600px;width:100%;height:auto;"><figcaption>Proposed launch FAQ. **What finances the next gate?** Actual contribution, capped founder funds or a separately agreed financing tranche.
